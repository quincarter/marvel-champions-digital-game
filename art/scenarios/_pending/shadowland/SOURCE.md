# Shadowland story panels (pending)

Five panel artworks from the bottom of Hall of Heroes' Shadowland page (https://hallofheroeslcg.com/shadowland/),
saved by the owner on 2026-10-20. The Shadowland insert has no comic page, so these are the raw material for a
story/intro sequence for the scenario. They move to `art/scenarios/<scenarioId>/` (or a story folder) once the
scenario's id exists. Artwork © 2026 Marvel; Marvel Champions is Fantasy Flight Games'.

- `panel-1-shadowland-daredevil.png`: Daredevil in black (the Shadowland look) amid Hand ninjas; the cover-style key art
- `panel-2-daredevil.png`: Daredevil wrapped in pink flame
- `panel-3-throne.png`: a hooded figure on a stone throne, purple energy
- `panel-4-hand-ninja.png`: a masked ninja with sai in purple smoke
- `panel-5-dojo.png`: an empty dojo corridor with a great gear

## Comic pages (removed)

Six pages from Marvel's 2010 _Shadowland_ event comic were added on 2026-10-20 and removed the same day (owner): full
interior comic pages are not redistributed from this public repository or its builds. A story or Extras screen can
credit and link the issue instead.
