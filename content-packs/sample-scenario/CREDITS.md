# Credits

The Clockmaker is a sample scenario pack written for the Marvel Champions Digital repository. The villain, the
schemes, the card names and the card text are original to this pack. No Marvel or Fantasy Flight Games art or
characters are used, and the pack has no art.

Each card's ability is modeled on a base-game card, named in a comment in `abilities/index.ts`: Rhino (II), The
Break-In!, Shocker, Breakin' & Takin', Hard to Keep Down, Armored Rhino Suit, Bomb Scare and Hydra Bomber. The
scenario's record names the base game's Standard and Expert encounter sets; they are not copied into the pack.

Marvel Champions: The Card Game is the property of Fantasy Flight Games and Marvel.
