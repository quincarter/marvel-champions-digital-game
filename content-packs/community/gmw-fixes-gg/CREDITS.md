# Credits

GMW Fixes (`gmw-fixes-gg`) is **Gideon's Gaming**'s set of fixes for The Galaxy's Most Wanted campaign, rebuilt as full
cards by **Bluefire**.

- The errata: Gideon's Gaming, https://gideonsgaming.com/marvel-champions-fixing-galaxys-most-wanted/
- Rebuilt card faces (Hitch card templates over the official scans), English and Spanish, and the Spanish translation:
  Bluefire. 11 faces: Collector (I-III), The Grand Collection 1A/1B, Nebula's Ship, the Milano, Interception Imminent
  1A/1B and Take What Is Mine 2A/2B.
- Source version: 1.0 (approved 2026-11-03), in BluefireF5ran's curated repository
  https://github.com/bluefireF5ran/mc-digital-custom-packs at commit `a51e50da556ece0b0fe9a75848320416b8794cef` (folder
  `gmw_fixes_gg`: `overrides.json`, `alt-art/`).
- The rules themselves are the game's own Balanced option (issue #609), scripted by this project; the pack switches the
  option on and brings the rebuilt faces.

Marvel Champions: The Card Game, its cards and their art are the property of Fantasy Flight Games and Marvel.
