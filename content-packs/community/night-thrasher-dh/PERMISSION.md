# Permission

DesignHacker, the author of Night Thrasher, gave permission for this pack to be distributed through the mod store.

- Status: granted
- Scope: inclusion (the curated source's `mod.json` records no wider scope; see the README's open items for the Spanish
  translation)
- Date: not recorded in the curated source
- Via: BluefireF5ran's curated repository https://github.com/bluefireF5ran/mc-digital-custom-packs, at commit
  `9ce0a25ec5b1b6f08b3a926838d9295babe69758` (folder `night_thrasher_dh`, `mod.json`: `permission`).

**Evidence:** a Discord DM, 2026-11-04. BluefireF5ran wrote to DesignHacker that the project was starting to bring Night
Thrasher into the game, thanking him "for letting us use your work"; DesignHacker answered the same day: "No problem!
Thank you for YOURS!", adding that the pack is sealed by the community, so he can't change cards, but will clear up how
they are meant to work. The message does not mention the Spanish translation; that rests on the owner's confirmation of
2026-11-13 (`pack.json` `permission.scope`). The screenshots, shared by the project owner on 2026-11-15, are not kept in
the repository; the registry review can ask the owner for them.

**Store approval:** granted (`pack.json` `permission`). The author(s) granted it in the Marvel Champions homebrew channel on Discord; the project owner, Quin Carter, confirmed it on 2026-11-13.
