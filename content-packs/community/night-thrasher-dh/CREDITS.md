# Credits

Night Thrasher (`night-thrasher-dh`) is a fan-made pack by **DesignHacker**.

- Card design and art direction: DesignHacker.
- Card template: Hitch (the Hitch card templates).
- Spanish translation and layout: Francisco Merchan. Official Spanish terms are used where they exist (the hero is
  "Destructor Nocturno", Panini's name); the skate trick names stay in English.
- Pictures: en, es (base language en). `alt-art/` holds the author's alternate, masked alter-ego (Dwayne Taylor).
- Source version: "MC4 Sealed" with the author's errata of 2026-11-04, curated by BluefireF5ran in
  https://github.com/bluefireF5ran/mc-digital-custom-packs (commit `9ce0a25ec5b1b6f08b3a926838d9295babe69758`).
- Ability scripts, the suggested deck (`decks/night-thrasher-dh-aggression.json`, assembled for this game, not the
  author's) and the conversion to the pack format: this project.

The card data was converted from MarvelCDB-shaped records with the mod SDK's `importMarvelCdbPack`.
Character art is Marvel comics art, as used by the author.

Marvel Champions: The Card Game is the property of Fantasy Flight Games and Marvel.
