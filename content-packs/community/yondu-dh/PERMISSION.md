# Permission

DesignHacker (the hero) and BlueHG (the nemesis set), the authors of Yondu, gave permission for this pack to be
distributed through the mod store.

- Status: granted
- Scope: inclusion
- DesignHacker: confirmed.
- BlueHG: DesignHacker answered by DM that BlueHG will be OK with it (2026-11-11). Not confirmed by BlueHG directly.
- Via: BluefireF5ran's curated repository https://github.com/bluefireF5ran/mc-digital-custom-packs, at commit
  `a0c9f6000293115a9ecd58da347468ec875d52bb` (folder `yondu_dh`, `mod.json`: `permission`).

**Evidence:** a Discord DM, 2026-11-11. BluefireF5ran told DesignHacker that Yondu was in the first batch for the game,
pointed to the notes BluefireF5ran left in the pack's forum thread, and asked for BlueHG's OK as well ("I'd like to have his ok as well
before including it"). DesignHacker answered: "Oh, he'll be OK with it." The screenshots, shared by the project owner on
2026-11-15, are not kept in the repository; the registry review can ask the owner for them.

- DesignHacker: raised no objection to Yondu in that exchange, and had already granted the use of his work for Night
  Thrasher on 2026-11-04. His words in the screenshot are about BlueHG, not an explicit yes for Yondu itself.
- BlueHG: **still secondhand.** The screenshot is DesignHacker saying BlueHG "will be OK with it"; there is no message
  from BlueHG directly. Until BlueHG confirms directly (or someone with that message adds it here), the nemesis set's
  permission rests on DesignHacker's word.

**Store approval:** granted (`pack.json` `permission`). The author(s) granted it in the Marvel Champions homebrew channel on Discord; the project owner, Quin Carter, confirmed it on 2026-11-13.
