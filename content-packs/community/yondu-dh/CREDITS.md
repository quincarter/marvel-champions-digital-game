# Credits

Yondu (`yondu-dh`) is a fan-made pack by **DesignHacker** (the hero) and **BlueHG** (the nemesis set).

- Card design and art direction: DesignHacker (hero), BlueHG (nemesis set: Sav'rkk, Sword-Gun-Arm, Price on Your Head,
  Hard to Kill, Savage Destruction).
- Card template: Hitch (the Hitch card templates).
- Spanish translation and layout: Francisco Merchán. "Ravager" is "Saqueador" (the Spain term); the ability names
  (D'ast Whistlin', Crew Cut, Whistlin') stay in English.
- Pictures: en, es (base language en).
- Source version: "MC4 Sealed" (cost arrows added to Invest and Ravage 2026-09-01) plus the suggested errata of
  2026-11-11 (D'ast Whistlin', Whistle While You Work, the Hard to Kill flavor typo), curated by BluefireF5ran in
  https://github.com/bluefireF5ran/mc-digital-custom-packs (commit `a0c9f6000293115a9ecd58da347468ec875d52bb`).
- Ability scripts and the conversion to the pack format: this project. The precon (`decks/yondu-dh-aggression.json`)
  is the authors' own starter deck, converted.

The card data was converted from MarvelCDB-shaped records with the mod SDK's `importMarvelCdbPack`.
Character art is Marvel comics art, as used by the authors.

Marvel Champions: The Card Game is the property of Fantasy Flight Games and Marvel.
