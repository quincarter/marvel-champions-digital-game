# Credits

Throg (`throg-ptt`) is a fan-made pack by **Patatasaurio** and **Caurel**.

- Card design and art direction: Patatasaurio and Caurel.
- Card template: G.Triskelion (the G.Triskelion card templates).
- Languages: **Spanish is the authors' canonical version** (the base language of the pictures); the English text and
  pictures are a translation by Francisco Merchán.
- Pictures: en, es (base language es).
- Source version: the authors' files plus the curators' rules fixes of 2026-10-30 and 2026-11-03 (sent to the authors on
  2026-10-30 and 2026-11-07), curated by BluefireF5ran in https://github.com/bluefireF5ran/mc-digital-custom-packs
  (commit `a51e50da556ece0b0fe9a75848320416b8794cef`, folder `throg_ptt`).
- Ability scripts, the conversion to the pack format: this project. The starter deck
  (`decks/throg-ptt-justice.json`) is the curators' Justice deck (commit `d9be74f`), converted.

The card data was converted from MarvelCDB-shaped records with the mod SDK's `importMarvelCdbPack`.
Character art is Marvel comics art, as used by the authors.

Marvel Champions: The Card Game is the property of Fantasy Flight Games and Marvel.
