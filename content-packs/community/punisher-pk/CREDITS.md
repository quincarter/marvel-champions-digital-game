# Credits

The Punisher (`punisher-pk`) is a fan-made scenario pack by **PermaKarma**.

- Card design and art direction: PermaKarma.
- Card template: Hitch (the Hitch card templates).
- Translation, layout and the English and Spanish rebuild of the cards on the Hitch templates from the author's art:
  Francisco Merchan.
- Rules and wording fixes (the Punisher's attacks on minions, the removed loop, templates, Rachel Cole-Alves): the curators
  of https://github.com/bluefireF5ran/mc-digital-custom-packs. Rachel Cole-Alves is their proposal, not the author's card.
- Pictures: en, es (base language en).
- Source version: V2.5 plus the curators' fixes of 2026-11-12, at commit `a0c9f6000293115a9ecd58da347468ec875d52bb`.
- Ability scripts and the conversion to the pack format: this project.

The card data was converted from MarvelCDB-shaped records with the mod SDK's `importMarvelCdbPack`.
Character art is Marvel comics art, as used by the author.

Marvel Champions: The Card Game is the property of Fantasy Flight Games and Marvel.
