# Credits

Mephisto (`mephisto-nep`) is a fan-made scenario pack by **Neptune** (Typhon00).

- Card design, text and art direction: Neptune ("MC Custom Content by: Neptune" is printed on every card).
- Constructive feedback (named on the pack insert): crimsonstorm2265, Kajislav, Lord_Havok, Runboy, ZeroZXL.
- AI disclosure, as printed on the insert: "This creation used AI tools as part of its development to achieve it proof of
  concept status. This primarily consisted of editing the existing artworks to expand imagery and remove text boxes.
  This content is designed as free, generating no profit for myself or Marvel comics."
- Pictures: en only (base language en). They are converted from the author's PNGs by `convert-art.cjs`. The eight
  pictures in `alt-art/` are the author's new art for the base game's Standard (01186-01190) and Expert (01191-01193)
  encounter cards.
- Source: the author's download "Mephisto" (87 files: scenario pack, Standard and Expert art, four modular sets, the
  printouts and the rules insert), shared by the owner. The author distributes it in the community Discord.
- Card data transcription, the conversion to the pack format and the README's rules summary: this project (see
  `TRANSCRIPTION.md` for what was read by eye and is unconfirmed).

The card template looks like the Hitch card templates used by the other community packs; the source does not say so.

Marvel Champions: The Card Game is the property of Fantasy Flight Games and Marvel. Character art is Marvel comics art,
as used by the author.
