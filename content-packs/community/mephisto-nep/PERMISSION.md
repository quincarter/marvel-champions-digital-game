# Permission

Neptune (Typhon00), the author of the Mephisto scenario pack, gave permission for this pack to be used in the game and
distributed through its Mod Store.

- Status: granted
- Scope: inclusion in the game and its Mod Store (the pack's cards, its encounter sets and the Standard and Expert art).
- Date: 2026-11-02
- By: Neptune (Typhon00), by direct message to the community member Dreadheart (the community Discord is where the
  creator distributes his work).
- Evidence: a screenshot of the creator's message, shared by the owner, in which he says his content "is free to play,
  download, print, what have you". The owner confirmed on 2026-11-12 that permission is granted (the screenshot as first
  shared was cut off). The screenshot is not kept in the repository; the registry review can ask the owner for it.

The pack insert's own credit line reads: "MC Custom Content by: Neptune. ... This content is designed as free,
generating no profit for myself or Marvel comics."

**Store approval:** granted (`pack.json` `permission.status`), so the publish workflow publishes it. The record there
(who, date, evidence, scope and the languages the grant covers) mirrors this file.
