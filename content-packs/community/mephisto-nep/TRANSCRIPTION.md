# Transcription notes: what is unconfirmed

Everything was read from the author's PNGs (`Scenario Pack/`) and cross-read against the A4 printout sheets and the
rules insert. There is no second text source (no MarvelCDB or Hall of Heroes entry exists for a fan pack), so "unsure"
means "read by eye, one source". Card text is word for word, printed typos included. Markup follows the base data:
`[star]` for a printed star before an ability, `[per_hero]`, `[boost]`, `[wild]`, `[crisis]`, `[acceleration]`; trait
names in text are title case ("Bargain attachment"), trait fields upper case.

Conventions chosen (not uncertainty): flavor text is stored where the schema has a field (treachery, side scheme,
environment) and left out for villain and main scheme stages (no field). Italic flavor on the main scheme faces and the
villain stage I quote ("No no no, I don't want just your soul. I want everything that makes it worthwhile.") are
transcribed here only. Reminder text the base cards print for icons ("Hazard Icon: Deal +1 encounter card ...") is not on
these cards, so it is not in the data.
