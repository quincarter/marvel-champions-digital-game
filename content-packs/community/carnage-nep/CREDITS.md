# Credits

Carnage (`carnage-nep`) is a fan-made pack by **Neptune**.

- Card design and art direction: Neptune.
- Card template: Hitch (the Hitch card templates).
- Spanish translation: Francisco Merchan. Official Spanish terms are used where they exist.
- Pictures: en, es (base language en). The 11 basic cards in `alt-art/` are official cards with the author's art.
- Source version: 3.0 (author update 2026-11-08), curated by BluefireF5ran in
  https://github.com/bluefireF5ran/mc-digital-custom-packs (commit `9ce0a25ec5b1b6f08b3a926838d9295babe69758`).
- Ability scripts, the suggested deck (`decks/carnage-nep-aggression.json`, assembled for this game, not the author's)
  and the conversion to the pack format: this project.

The card data was converted from MarvelCDB-shaped records with the mod SDK's `importMarvelCdbPack`.
Character art is Marvel comics art, as used by each author.

Marvel Champions: The Card Game is the property of Fantasy Flight Games and Marvel.
