# Credits

Lost Traits (`lost-traits-bf`) is an optional house rule by **Bluefire**, from an idea by **Borja** on the Digital
Discord server. The server's poll approved it (2026-11).

- The rule: thirteen official heroes and alter-egos gain one trait. The criterion is a trait another official card of
  the same character already has (Web-Warrior, Avenger, Aerial, Defender, Mutant), plus Martial Artist (the Fear No Evil
  trait) for Black Widow, Black Panther, Gamora and Psylocke. Spanish "Artista marcial" is Bluefire's wording (no
  official Spanish printing of the trait yet).
- Rebuilt card faces, English and Spanish: Bluefire, on the Hitch card templates over the official scans (the footer
  "1A\*" marks an edited official card).
- Source version: 1.2 (2026-11-13), in BluefireF5ran's curated repository
  https://github.com/bluefireF5ran/mc-digital-custom-packs at commit `a51e50da556ece0b0fe9a75848320416b8794cef` (folder
  `lost_traits_bf`: `overrides.json`, `alt-art/`).
- The conversion to the pack format: this project.

Marvel Champions: The Card Game, its cards and their art are the property of Fantasy Flight Games and Marvel.
