# Credits

Sample Sentinel is a sample pack written for the Marvel Champions Digital repository. The character, the card names,
the card text and the placeholder art (`art/hero.svg`) are original to this pack. No Marvel or Fantasy Flight Games art
or characters are used.

Each card's ability is modeled on a base-game card, named in a comment in `abilities/index.ts`. The deck in `decks/`
lists base-game card codes, which a deck may do; the cards themselves are not copied into the pack.

Marvel Champions: The Card Game is the property of Fantasy Flight Games and Marvel.
