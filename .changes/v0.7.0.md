## [v0.7.0] - 2026-09-25
### Added
* Attack and Thwart ask which character goes when more than one can (hero and allies), showing each one's stat, consequential damage, and a stun or confuse that would cancel it, so you choose the order; the button shows how many can go and keeps the stun hatch
* Rhino one-off intro: before the deal, a standalone Rhino game opens on a comic artboard that pans to Spider-Man, then Rhino, then the whole scene, with captions and Spider-Man quips (narration when he isn't at the table); SKIP goes straight to the deal
### Changed
* Rhino intro: on tablets and desktops each quip's bubble sits beside its speaker over a quiet part of the art, with a tail pointing at them; phones keep bubbles under the art
### Fixed
* Tapping a hero, ally or other card in play opens it in Inspect instead of using its ability, so a stray tap no longer exhausts it; abilities are used from the card's Inspect buttons
* An exhausted ally that also took damage in the same attack (e.g. consequential damage) no longer slides off the bottom of the play area on phones
### Engine
* Pepper Potts copies the top of the discard pile as it stood before the payment, so a card spent from hand in the same payment no longer replaces it (FAQ Pepper Potts #33, RRG 1.8 p. 58); paying with Pepper on an Energy plus another card now works
