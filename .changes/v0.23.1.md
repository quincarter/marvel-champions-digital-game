## [v0.23.1] - 2026-10-17
### Fixed
* The defend prompt's damage range now counts prevention already on the attack, such as Magic Barrier's "prevent 3" (#212).
* A decision sheet no longer keeps a card picked on a sheet that was undone: after an undo, the end-of-turn discard could hold an invisible pick that stopped the hand's last card from being chosen (#211).
