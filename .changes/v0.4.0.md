## [v0.4.0] - 2026-09-22
### Added
* Campaign mode step 10: CampaignStorage/IdbCampaignStorage (a third IndexedDB database, mc-campaigns) and SaveMeta.campaignId/campaignNodeId (SAVE_SCHEMA 4, migrating a pre-campaign save rather than retiring it)
* Campaign mode step 11: the five client view models over campaign state (browser list, log sheet, between-games step/launch/fold, deck-edit gate, scenario-choice board), and SessionConfig.campaign wiring a composed campaign game through the existing host path
### Fixed
* Client: the game no longer freezes when a hero is defeated (or any HP/threat counter is mid-count during a redraw): the counter's animation is tied to what it draws, and one failing frame can no longer stop the game loop
### Engine
* Campaign mode C1: a composable mode set (PlayModes) replaces the standard/expert difficulty enum; existing saves replay byte-for-byte
* Campaign mode C1: the campaign definition and campaign log type vocabulary, plain serializable data, with a synthetic fixture campaign
* Campaign mode C1: in-game campaign primitives — log reads in card effects, recordInCampaignLog and removeFromCampaign (by card face, per ruling April 30, 2026), campaign input frozen into GameState, five campaign setup windows and trace events
* Campaign runner: resolveBetweenGames, startGameFromLog, campaignResultOf and applyCampaignResult, with seeded campaign randomness and replayable pending-choice re-entry
* Deck rules in campaign context: validateDeck takes a DeckContext, so a campaign-specific card is legal inside its own campaign, removed and prohibited cards are refused, the identity is locked, a frozen deck cannot be edited, and campaign grants are exempt from deck size
* Campaign vocabulary: filtered campaign-set choices, a tucked-cards record query, optional random draws, a choiceMade predicate (and the flag polarity fix), and deck legality for campaign-granted scenario cards and obligations
* Add ValueSpec deckCount (deckCountOf in the DSL): a player's deck size, so "the top half of their deck" can feed a zone selector's top
### Content
* Campaign mode C1: the Campaign content record (id, box/pack code, scenarios, campaign-specific sets, log-sheet reference), validateCampaign, and The Rise of Red Skull's (MC10) record — the only campaign box with scenario data ingested so far
* Campaign mode C1 step 7: The Rise of Red Skull (MC10) CampaignDefinition — five scenarios as a linear graph, the campaign registry, and the coverage/smoke tests; four printed instructions left as flagged vocabulary-gap TODOs
* Script 28 of MC10's 30 Hydra Campaign ability refs (TECH upgrades, both faces of the four Condition upgrades, Zola's Algorithm, and Medical Emergency); Martial Law and Anti-Hero Propaganda stay skipped pending a content-data ref split
* Split Martial Law (04165) and Anti-Hero Propaganda (04166) into constant/action ability refs via a generalized obligation-parser rule, and script all 30 of MC10's Hydra Campaign refs
* The Rise of Red Skull (MC10) rules-fidelity QA suite: full standard/expert campaigns, a real driven villain-defeat win, retry/removal/legality/determinism scenarios, each test cited to MC10, RRG, or a post-1.7 ruling
* Campaign mode step 12: MC21 acceptance gate passes content-only (test-only definition + one DSL parameter); one deck-size primitive gap flagged for game-rules-architect
* Campaign gate (MC21): 'discard the top half of your deck' rounds down, matching the printed MC21 card rather than the RRG modifier rule; flagged for FFG
