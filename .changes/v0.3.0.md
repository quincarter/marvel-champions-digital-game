## [v0.3.0] - 2026-09-22
### Added
* Client: Ctrl/Cmd+Shift+D copies a diagnostic snapshot of the running scenes, overlays and pending decision, for bug reports
### Changed
* Client: on desktop the Inspect card panel and its art grow with the window, and a long table's villain and scheme rows grow into a tall band so the cards read larger
* Client: phone Scenario select and Take your seats reclaim the top of the screen — the search field is behind a toggle at the head of a scrollable chip rail, and the active seat's details collapse into a disclosure
* Client: aspect filter chips wear the aspect's card-frame colour, matching the stamps on the hero cards
* Engine: the 'can't afford' refusal reads as a sentence ("Needs 1 resource; the payment covers 0.") instead of printing the requirement as JSON
* Client: aspect filter chips are full-colour aspect stamps, matching the stamps on the hero cards, and every filter chip and aspect stamp uses the Bangers comic face (Take your seats, Scenario select, Decks)
### Fixed
* Client: the pending-choice sheet comes back when the engine answers one decision and raises the next in the same command (targets, triggers, the villain phase); it no longer sits faded out until the game is resumed
* Client: the setup screens' search fields are visible again (they were hidden under the always-running music scene)
* Client: cards you may play from outside your hand (an Arrow on Hawkeye's Quiver, a card playable from your discard) appear at the end of the hand strip, tagged with where they are
* Client: an overlay that is closing no longer takes clicks, the villain-phase walkthrough takes none while the choice sheet owns a decision, and an answered choice sheet left on an unchanged decision gives it back after a moment instead of ignoring clicks until a reload
### Engine
* A Boost that makes the same villain activate again (The Wrecking Crew's I've Been Waiting For This!) no longer re-flips its own boost card forever; the villain phase used to freeze on that command
* Resources that only pay for one kind of card (Expert Marksman: a resource for an Arrow event) now count when the game decides whether you can afford that card
* Arrow events attached to Hawkeye's Quiver are offered as legal plays (the legal-move search looked only one attachment level deep)
