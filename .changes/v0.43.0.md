## [v0.43.0] - 2026-11-02
### Added
* The Galaxy's Most Wanted can be started on an opt-in Balanced house rule, based on fixes by Gideon's Gaming and requested by Borja, with four optional toggles and full points
* A new achievement, The Galaxy Is Unfair, for winning a Balanced Galaxy's Most Wanted campaign
* Heroic and Skirmish modes: set a heroic level or play one villain version, on Table setup and for each campaign issue. Suggested by @zackburnley.
* Campaign pages can draw the box art faded behind their side panel, with a Faded art on campaign pages switch in Settings
* Settings has a Fade color choice of grey, blue, red, purple or green for the side panel on campaign pages
* A Balanced toggle adds Kree Supremacy to Ronan's deck with 2 or more heroes, in place of the optional reveal
* A Balanced Galaxy's Most Wanted run shows bluefireF5ran's edited card scans in English and Spanish, and Inspect shows their text
* Add Doppelganger and Arch-enemy trophies, and a trophy for every team-up pair that opens the pair's art in Extras (#615, suggested by @PermaKarma).
* The v0.43.0 release report has its own page on the site, at /releases/v0.43.0/
### Changed
* The Briefing's deck and scheme column sits on the same coloured pane as the Balanced prompt, with each campaign's box art faded behind it
### Fixed
* Breakout (The Wrecking Crew): a player card that names "the villain" means the active villain, with no choice of villain (insert, "The Active Villain"; FFG ruling Jun 13, 2024)
* A piercing attack whose damage is dealt as indirect damage or divided among characters now pierces only the attacked character (the defender, or the identity if undefended); other characters keep their tough status cards, and piercing granted to that attack alone now reaches the attacked character (RRG 1.8 pp. 24, 32).
* A lasting effect on a card (Goliath's +4 ATK until the end of the phase) no longer applies to that card once it leaves play and comes back as a new copy
* A choice the enemy team must make with only one legal target (Colleen Wing, Daredevil) resolves without asking the first player
* A quickstrike or teamwork activation started inside an enemy's activation now always waits for that activation to finish, even when nothing follows the effect that put the minion into play (RRG 1.8 p. 6).
* A card scan missing from a build falls back to the normal scan rather than a generated frame
* A press on the Briefing's deck rows, role tiles and pick tiles needs a real tap, so a swipe that lifts on one no longer opens it
* Breakout (The Wrecking Crew): with a 'Pool deck, the Dreadpool set stays set aside at setup until Crisis of Infinite Deadpools is revealed, instead of coming into play with the four signature side schemes (#619, reported by @bluefireF5ran)
* Breakout (The Wrecking Crew): a Dreadpool card that has been shuffled in or revealed discards to the active villain's pile, instead of always Wrecker's (#619)
* Game log: a boost card is named, a boost card that attaches itself (Red Skull's Luger) says so, and a ranged attack that ignores retaliate says why no retaliate damage followed (#623)
### Engine
* A mainSchemeValue scenario rule changes a named main scheme stage's values, and a campaign log can start with seeded hidden fields
