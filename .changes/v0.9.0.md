### Changed
* Seats and Scenario select scroll smoothly: roster card faces are painted in a web worker (art fetched, decoded and downscaled off the main thread), and scrolling moves the shelves instead of redrawing them
### Fixed
* A roster card's SELECTED / LOCKED / SEAT tag no longer runs past its red plate on desktop
### Engine
* "Then" now also waits on searches and player-deck "discard until" that find nothing, reveals with no card or a cancelled one, cancels with nothing to cancel, and attacks or schemes that don't happen (preThenUnresolved on the log).
* "Each X attacks/schemes. Then, …" with no X in play counts as resolved, so its "then" still runs.
* "Discard cards from the encounter deck until …" now stops when the encounter deck empties instead of carrying on into the reshuffled deck (RRG Encounter Deck).
