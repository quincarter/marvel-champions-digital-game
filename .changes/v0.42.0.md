## [v0.42.0] - 2026-11-01
### Added
* A deck's card list sorts by aspect, type, cost or name, in the builder and in Deck check, and the choice is remembered (#594)
* Review in the deck builder opens Deck check on the deck as edited, saved or not (#595)
* Twenty-three more cards show their Spanish scans, including SP//dr's Peni Parker and pilot upgrade
* The v0.42.0 release report has its own page on the site, at /releases/v0.42.0/
### Fixed
* Card lists show every card that shares a name: the seven Spider-Man allies, the Spider-Women and others no longer hide behind one row (#598)
* Card search matches a card's subtitle, so "otto" finds Spider-Man (Otto Octavius) (#596)
* Searching "spdr" finds SP//dr (#597)
* SP//dr Suit's support side and the SP//dr pilot upgrade show their scans in built games, in every card language (#599)
* Ending a turn with an empty hand no longer asks about a small hand size, as with SP//dr (#600)
* Paying with SP//dr's Sync Ratio shows the Interface card each option exhausts (#601)
