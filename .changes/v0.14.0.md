## [v0.14.0] - 2026-10-01
### Added
* Export all your saved games, decks, campaigns and unlocks to a file from Settings → Save data, and import it on another device.
* The deck builder can filter the card pool by pack and by wave, and sort it by name, cost or pack
### Fixed
* Beat Cop, Mantis and Fruition (printed "Action:") can now be used in alter-ego form, and Cloak of Levitation (printed "Hero Action:") only in hero form.
* Moment of Triumph now offers its heal after your hero attacks and defeats an enemy; before, it was never offered.
* Locked and Loaded (Venom) can be played in alter-ego form, as its printed plain Action allows
* White Tiger (Spectrum pack) draws her cards when played in alter-ego form too, as her printed plain Response allows
* Preservation (Vision pack) heals 1 damage from your hero when spent; it healed nothing before
* Audacity (Valkyrie pack) no longer deals damage when spent in alter-ego form; it prints a Hero Response
* Pasting a decklist that names a card Sinister Motives reprints (such as Young Love) imports it instead of reporting an ambiguous name
* Importing a Spider-Woman or Adam Warlock deck from MarvelCDB reads all its chosen aspects instead of refusing the deck
* Anticipation (Valkyrie pack) can now interrupt a minion engaging you, including one revealed in the villain phase
* Interrupt and Response events attached to Jocasta, George Stacy or the Black Panther ally can be played in their timing window, as their text allows
* Shadow of the Past shuffles only your nemesis set into the encounter deck; it took every set-aside card, including Ironheart's later identity versions, which then hung the game when revealed
* Deck builder on phones: the identity list scrolls by drag, the deck controls scroll in their own area so the card pool stays on screen, and a drag no longer leaves a button highlighted.
* Deck builder: an aspect's tip opens over the aspect buttons instead of under them, and the aspect buttons wear their aspect's colour, as on the hero selection screen.
### Engine
* A revealed minion's Quickstrike attack now resolves before its When Revealed ability, per FFG's Feb 28, 2026 ruling
### Content
* Venom (Eddie Brock) and Symbiote Suit, Sinister Motives' two basic cards outside a hero kit, now work in custom decks instead of doing nothing.
* Marvel Boy's card text names the physical resource icon instead of MarvelCDB's typo "[physics]"
### Docs
* Commit messages, PRs and GitHub comments carry no Claude attribution; a commit-msg hook strips it from commit messages
* Cross-hero tests prove the aspect cards of Core and waves 1-3 play from another hero's deck
* A real MarvelCDB decklist for each of the 29 Core–wave 4 heroes imports, is legal and plays a full game in the test suite
