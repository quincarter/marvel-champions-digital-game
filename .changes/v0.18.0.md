### Engine
* A script can raise a named moment that other cards answer (raiseMoment, on.moment)
* Finding and revealing a card that is already in play: a minion engages the finder and keeps what is on it; a side scheme or attachment stays where it is
* A card in play can be dealt to a player as a facedown encounter card, and a dealt card can be passed to the next player; the reveal step repeats until no dealt card remains
* An ability can ask whether a card of a given type was among the cards that paid for it (paidWithCard)
* A rule can keep the top card of a player's deck faceup for everyone, with a log line each time the showing card changes
* A constant can make the top card of a player's deck playable as if it were in hand, at a reduced cost, once per phase
* A payment records how many resources and which resource types paid; the player declares a wild resource's type when a card reads it
* An ability can cost a chosen number of resources within a range, and its payment's wild types are declared the same way a played card's are
* An attack-labeled ability is one attack: every instance of damage it deals to enemies is damage from that attack, retaliate answers once per surviving enemy after the last instruction, and 'after this attack' waits for the whole ability (Q47)
* An additional cost to change form (formChangeCost): the turn's change carries a payment, a player card's change-form effect asks for it, encounter-card changes are free; Jubilee's Grounded now charges 2 resources of the same type to change to hero form
* Every ability labeled (attack) is one attack even when it only deals damage (Q48); guard is checked for each enemy an attack ability targets as it would be attacked (Q49); attacked targets are tracked apart from damage recipients, so overkill victims are not attacked and each targeted enemy is (Q50)
* discardDeckUntil can bind every card it discarded (bindAll), and a raised moment can carry named slots to the abilities that answer it (raiseMoment carry, read as moment.<slot>)
* A card can have a chosen player make a basic attack or thwart with a bonus (basicPowerBy, canUseBasicPower); Jubilee's Cell Phone is registered
* Engine pieces for five held cards: a target chosen by the ability label it prints (printsAbility), attach hosts by classification, a cost that readies a card, upgrades that can attach to an ally, and a deck-discard cost of a chosen size
* Villains in a random row with the active counter passing to the next in the row (Four Horsemen, Q5); setup options applied after step 11 (Gene Pool's extra threat); a flipped card's event names the player who flipped it; setup step 11 resolves before step 12
* A character can be considered to have at least 1 hit point (defeat held off until the floor ends); a card's Forced Response can be resolved as a cost or effect with no attack made; lasting effects can run until the next villain phase begins
* A villain's next stage can be revealed by an effect with no defeat; the printed hit point numeral is readable; named abilities can be ignored by a rule
* A Special can be resolved as a cost with the player choosing among several Setting environments; a villain can change form with no reveal step; an attachment's Forced Interrupt can be resolved as if its condition were met
* A scenario play area: cards in play that no player controls, closed to other cards' abilities unless an ability reaches it; allies can be played into it uncontrolled and blank
* Mission attempts: discarded cards paired one each with characters by resource icon, a damage pool dealt one enemy at a time, a card that cannot be discarded, a cost reduction for the next ally played to an area, a resource icon a character is considered to have; a side scheme held at no threat is defeated when its protection ends
* A title can be barred from entering play for a game; a card found at campaign setup counts toward the starting hand; a card's other face can be named by a query; a campaign's random draw can differ on each attempt at a node; a granted card's deck-size rule is data
* An attack's damage bonus applies to each qualifying instance of the attack ability and never twice to damage marked additional (Q53); a window before an identity changes form; hand-discard costs in response windows; last-known target state for consequential damage; surge granted to another card; an interrupt completed while its window is open is still offered
* Small engine pieces for the last held wave 8 cards: a card-attached trigger, cards drawn bound for an effect, a cost pick's host and stats, a chosen damage cost, a remaining hit points query, an enemy attack's total ATK, a mid-reveal card given as a boost card, a thwart-in-progress predicate, several return-to-hand picks, a permanent upgrade answering its former host leaving play
* A basic power reports the stat that powers it (a thwart made with ATK is ATK) and bonuses to that power follow it (Q54); a character made the defender by an ability is making a basic defense (Q55); a main scheme's B side has no live abilities until the scheme turns to it (Q56)
* Tests for the Doctor Strange 'Pool community deck (MarvelCDB 34506): legal import, its four lines of play, and proof it cannot loop
* An attack ability on an older card begins its attack as the ability starts resolving, so 'when you attack' answers before its other instructions (RRG 1.8 p. 26)
* Owner rulings: Generation X on a divided thwart, Panicked Refugees revealed and replaced on draw, overpaying a chosen-size cost, a prompt for tied payments only when it matters, Grounded charges for an encounter card's Action, Cell Phone lets a divider divide
* Removed the unused returnedToScenarioDeck log event and its client log line.
* Card effects that count boost icons (Hex Bolt, Taskmaster, Crossbones' Machine Gun and the rest) now open the boost-icon counting window, so Chaos Control and Scarlet Witch's Crest can change those counts
* An obligation's "you" is the player holding it for a state-check condition and its resolver, for "only you may trigger", and for who dealt its damage or removed its threat (the defeating player); a crisis icon still does not stop an obligation's own ability
* A divided threat removal ("remove N threat from among schemes") adds the card's threat bonus once to each scheme given a share (RRG 1.8 p. 19; FAQ Shrink #11, p. 59)
* A card played into a scenario area (playCard.into) may now be an upgrade or a player side scheme, as well as an ally or a support; an event is still refused, with its reason.
