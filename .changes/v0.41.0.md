## [v0.41.0] - 2026-10-31
### Added
* Earn the official promo cards by winning (3 or 10 games as a hero, 3 wins against a villain or with an event), and pick them in Extras to replace a card's normal art.
* Choose what the hero tiles show on Take Your Seats: Game art, Card art, or Promo art once earned.
* Cards left: the encounter deck's list now starts with the villain's stages, in play and still to come, such as Hela A1 and A2.
* Settings has a search: the magnifier in the title bar finds any setting and anything on the pages under it (Rules, Unlocks and How it works, Save data, Controls, Your stats, How to play), best match first with the matched words marked, and a press goes straight there (#576)
### Changed
* The You / Everyone switch sits at the top of Your stats and shows in the deck builder even with no games, and Share my games turns sharing on from the stats page
### Fixed
* Eighteen scenario pack cards showed the wrong scan (a neighbouring stage, side or villain's copy), including Green Goblin III, Mojo II, Spiral II, Enchantress I, Piledriver B and Expert Kang I; each now shows its own card.
* Black Widow III, Batroc B and A.I.M. Interference (mental) showed the wrong scan; each now shows its own card, and Black Widow II, Batroc A, Baron Zemo's other three faces and the other A.I.M. Interference cards use my scans.
* The Once and Future Kang: completing a stage 3 now removes that Kang (II) and the stage at the end of the phase, every Kang's Dominion starts set aside, each completed stage tucks exactly one under stage 4A, and players join only once when that Kang is defeated in the same phase.
* Escaped Convict's boost makes no attack on an ordinary scheme, and still does after a scheme that a boost started during an attack (FFG ruling, May 16, 2024).
* Taskmaster's stage-flip splash shows his face instead of his chest (twelve other villains cropped the same way are fixed too), and a lost issue's Rewind shows the villain's own picture rather than a comic panel.
* A player card ability that only readies a card is no longer offered while that card cannot ready, so Cloak of Levitation is not spent for nothing under Unnatural Storm (#568)
* Team-Building Exercise's payment shows one tile per card Organizational Support can exhaust, keeps Confirm off until the payment pays, and Undo can take the move back
* Decision sheets show the counters each card in play holds, so two copies of a Uses card can be told apart
* A setting changed from Pause during a game now applies when you return to the table, without a reload.
* A controller and the keyboard reach the theme and language buttons on the title menu, and the focused top button and Read more show the button to press
* The controller e2e specs expect the full top row on the title menu
* Rank progress bars fill Hero Red in light and dark mode, and bar tracks, the deck cost chart and the opening-hand scroller stay readable in dark mode
* Android builds free disk space first, so a hosted runner no longer runs out while signing the APK
### Content
* Scenario pack scans: my own scans now replace the remaining older and low-resolution images in Green Goblin, The Wrecking Crew, Kang, The Hood, MojoMania, Trickster Takeover and Synthezoid Smackdown, plus the Standard PvP faces and the two Trickster Takeover reference cards.
* Add 128 promo card scans: 25 heroes, 12 villains and villain teams, and 5 events.
* Hela's Wounded faces now show their real card scans, and her expert Mystic side and The Wastes of Niffleheim use new scans.
### Docs
* The v0.40.0 release report images, and the report's final Code shipped count and tutorial wording
* Screenshots cover the iPhone SE, Pixel 10, tablet portrait and landscape, and desktop
