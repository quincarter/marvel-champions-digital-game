## [v0.40.0] - 2026-10-31
### Added
* Peek at table lets you switch the table to any seat's side, hand and all, and the team rail's View does the same outside a decision, so one player running several heroes can read a teammate's hand before deciding
* A choose-a-player decision has View table beside each player, with a number key and a controller combo, to read that seat's hand and table before choosing
* A decision listed as rows (choose a player, choose a target) shows the hero, villain, minion or scheme picture on each row, with the card hover over it
* A Your stats dashboard, opened from the Title, Decks & Collection, the Trophy Room, Settings and Pause: your record and streaks, how you lose, favorite heroes and toughest villains, side schemes, the devices you play on, and each hero's most played, most used and rarely played cards and favorite aspects
* Games finished before this update get their card numbers filled in once, by replaying their saved logs in the background, so Your stats and the deck builder count them too
* Your games now help build decks: Decks & Collection's From your games tab, a card's own numbers in the side pane and in Inspect, Build my own from a precon, and a Cards tab in Your stats
* Your stats now count cards drawn, resources made and the cards paid with, best turns, allies' damage, defenses and defeats while defending, encounter cards revealed, what hurt the heroes most, the final blow's card, and time played; older finished games are filled in once more
* Inspect on a hero identity steps through its Hero, Alter-Ego and extra form sides, so you can read a hero you don't own before picking it (#532).
* Deck builder search filters in the style of marvelcdb: o:2, o<3, t:ally|event, rp:1, k:spy, kw:guard, x:, f:, e:, y:, u:, atk/thw/h, with ! to exclude (#531)
* Deck builder quick filters: cost chips 0 to 5+, resource icon chips and a trait stepper, built for a tap on a phone (#531)
* The deck builder remembers its sort, quick and pack filters and card/list view between visits (#531)
* Settings › Gameplay › Signature cards (Free, Standard, Hidden) and Deck builder copy limits, for building to the standard deck rules (#531)
* Deck builder odds: the chance a draw holds 0, 1, 2 or 3+ of a group (a resource icon, resource cards, a type, a trait, a title), from the opening hand to Black Cat's top 2 (#531)
* Cards left: tap your deck to list the cards still in it with counts, never in order; the encounter deck's list and a boost average are optional settings (#540).
* Your stats shows win rates per villain, modular set and hero with why those games were lost, and the deck builder suggests cards from your winning and losing decks against each villain
* Opt in to share anonymous game results and see everyone's win rates and loss causes per villain, modular set and hero
* Deck builder search cheat sheet: a ? beside the search lists every term, how to combine them, and examples to use or copy (#531)
* The v0.40.0 release report has its own page on the site, at /releases/v0.40.0/, so the release notes can link to it
* Deck builder and deck check: a Side scheme filter chip finds player side schemes (#548).
* The board shows a Victory pile once a card reaches the victory display: tap it to see every card there (#548).

* A controller can walk a full play area row with the D-pad, and a sideways push of the right stick scrolls it from anywhere on the table
* The card scan importer no longer crashes when saving a scan without cleared corners over an old JPEG scan
* A Response to a minion entering play, such as Hawkeye's, resolves after its Quickstrike attack starts and before the attack's damage (ruling 137).
* The top-bar buttons no longer cover a choice popup's title on a phone, and the dark mode button no longer floats over Pause, Inspect and other popups (#514).
* The target picker starts below the table's top bar, so its title is no longer under the top-bar buttons, and the first player token no longer shows over it (#515).
* Bloody Mary's Assembled Armor shows it breaks at 5 damage in standard mode, not the expert 8 (#517, thanks @zackburnley).
* The loss screen says when Change of Venue swapped your main scheme, and names the scheme that actually won when each player has their own (#518, thanks @zackburnley).
* Protection Racket on standard lets each player choose their main scheme in Table setup, instead of always dealing Battle in the Bodega (#522, thanks @zackburnley).
* The scenario tiles' two-line captions (the Fear No Evil ones) fit on the tile instead of running off its bottom edge (#521, thanks @zackburnley).
* The keyword-order prompt after an each-time loop (Drang III) names each keyword's minion on its own line, tells two same-name minions apart by the order they were discarded, shows the minion on hover, and says why the order is asked.
* The declared-defender tip is worded for the hero or ally it names, and the defend sheet shows a keyword attack waiting for its ability as waiting.
* Deck builder: a card search no longer overrides the chosen sort, and a second Then sort key (plus Aspect, Type and Match sorts) orders its ties (#531)
* Export save data and Download log now open Android's own Save dialog in the Android app, where they did nothing before (#534).
* On Android the on-screen keyboard now lays over the game instead of shrinking it, in the browser and in the app (#537).
* Your stats: the card hover opens on a row's picture at a normal card size, no longer filling the screen from anywhere on the row
* Desktop: larger text in the From your games rail, the deck list's descriptions and the import box on Decks & Collection and the deck builder (#531)
* The table's card hover opens at a normal card size on a scheme, villain or hero, no longer scaled from the whole panel on a wide screen.
* Your stats e2e: the card hover spec reads the Heroes tab's stop correctly after #546
* Clicks outside the tutorial's current step now stay blocked until you use Skip this step, Stop tutorial or Escape, steps waiting on Got it hold the table, and a step that needs a card you skipped moves on by itself (#554)
### Engine
* A delayed effect no longer touches a card that left play and came back: the card is a new copy (RRG 1.8 Leaves Play, p. 27)
* Every constant modifier to the damage a character takes (caps such as Titanium Exoskeleton's and Boarding Party's, reductions and increases; not doubling) now applies before 'would take' interrupts such as Magnetic Bubble see the amount
* An ability that puts a Quickstrike minion into play and then makes an enemy attack resolves that attack in place, the rest of its text, then the Quickstrike (RRG 1.8 p. 6, ruling 194); inside an outer attack both wait for it.
* An ally that defends an attack no longer counts as using a basic power, so 'when you use a basic power' cards such as Melinda May are not triggered by it (FFG ruling Mar 16, 2025)
* A 'cannot be stunned' or 'cannot be confused' effect keeps a status card the character already holds; only gaining stalwart removes it (FFG ruling Feb 15, 2024, #145: Magneto's Helmet, Ronan the Accuser)
* The game log records each card a payment spends from hand, as the payment priced it, whichever payment it was (a play, an ability, a paid response, an effect)
* A hero's logged defeat names the card that defeated it and whether it was an attack
