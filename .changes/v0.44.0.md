## [v0.44.0] - 2026-11-12
### Added
* Translate the title menu into Spanish, the first screen on a new translation layer that falls back to English for text not yet translated (#390).
* Show the deck name you saved on the campaign roster's hero picker and seat cards, so two decks of one hero and aspect tell apart (#658)
* The v0.44.0 release report has its own page on the site, at /releases/v0.44.0/
### Fixed
* Crossbones' Machine Gun and Full Auto deal their indirect damage to the player who takes it, among their own characters, instead of letting the first player spread it over every hero.
* Avalanche! and Corrupted Timestream let each player choose their own option, instead of the player who revealed the card choosing for everyone.
* Widow's Bite is no longer offered for a minion that has already left play, so a second copy isn't spent for nothing after the first defeated it.
* Winter, Widow, Soldier, Spy no longer offers a "Max 1 per player" Preparation you already control, and still deals its damage when no Preparation in your discard pile can enter play.
* Moon Knight's draw-2 Response (Taskmaster's captives) now needs a [wild] resource, as printed, not any resource
* Hawkeye, Iceman and Prism Dust are offered only while the minion that entered play is still in play
* Time-Travel Tactics (damage and boost) and Kang (Master of Time) count the obligations in the right player's own play area
* Machine Man is no longer offered with nothing to spend, and a payment with nothing to pick always keeps its Don't use it button, so the game can't get stuck there (#654).
* A 7 in the comic number font now has a crossbar, so a villain at 7 hit points no longer reads as 1 (#660, reported by @Kravenprime).
* Save the Shawarma Place and Defensive Protocols give each player 1 copy of Shawarma or System Shock, not every copy (#663)
* Alias Investigations no longer spends its evidence counters on a villain that cannot be defeated, such as Proxima Midnight or Corvus Glaive while the other has hit points left (#663)
* After Psychic Misdirection, the defend sheet shows the attack's damage going to the chosen enemy, and that defending only takes your DEF off what it takes (#665).
* Pinned Down now places 2 threat for each obligation in play, as printed, instead of 1 (#675)
* Beating the Black Widow villain with Black Widow seated now earns only her Seeing Double trophy; Clearing the Ledger is for beating Taskmaster (#643)
* The Power Stone now gives whoever holds it its printed +1 ATK and +1 SCH or THW: Nebula and Ronan when it is on them, and a hero once it moves to them (#678).
* The Galaxy's Most Wanted Market no longer stops on an empty Decline-only prompt once every card of a price is bought, so a hero's cart is no longer lost on the way (#679).
* Ten more encounter attachments now give their printed stat boxes, which were missing: Blackjack's Bazooka, Drang's Spear, Universal Weapon, Cloak of Hercules, Challenge Accepted, Corvus's Glaive, Lethal Weapon, Humongous Hallucination, Masterful Mirage, Cyborg Tech and Pull of Power, and the SCH/THW attachments in Fear No Evil now count for THW on a hero (#678).
### Engine
* Berserker Barrage's repeat is its own attack: the enemy it hits is no longer also counted as attacked by the first, so it retaliates once, not twice
* Resolve an ally's "after uses a basic power" responses (Nick Fury) before its consequential damage, so they are still offered when that damage defeats it
* A (defense) ability from a player whose hero cannot defend the attack (Taunt) no longer stops the attacked hero from defending
* An attachment's stat boxes no longer count while its identity is in alter-ego form (an alter-ego has no ATK, THW or DEF), and count again on the flip back to hero (RRG v1.8 "Attachment", p. 8).
