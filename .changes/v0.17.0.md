### Engine
* Player side schemes show their printed scheme icons, count for scheme-icon totals, and leave play with an eliminated player's other cards
* A player side scheme can be put into play by an effect, with the same placement, controller and starting threat as when it is played
* The player side scheme limit: one for one or two players, two for three or four; over the limit a scheme is discarded undefeated
* Assault: an ally thwarting an assault scheme takes its ATK consequential damage, and a divided basic thwart that includes an assault scheme uses ATK
* Per player printed costs on player cards (Team Investigation, Break Time): one reader for a card's printed cost, scaled by the players who started
* A When Defeated that names 'the player who defeated this scheme' still resolves when no player did, with no player as its source
* A villain's defeated last stage can be placed under another card and counted there; a villain still in play is never tucked
* A 'shares a title with' card query and a general 'not' for card queries, on the unique rule's title comparison
* A card can say card abilities cannot remove it from play: moves and outright defeats from abilities do nothing, damage and game rules still remove it
* The main scheme records what advanced it (a completed stage or a card's effect), and a stage can ask
* Tests for an attachment or obligation blanking an identity's text box on both faces (traits and printed stats kept)
* An enemy attack as the cost of a player's ability; the ability is not offered while that enemy cannot attack
* A scenario whose villain is put into play by the main scheme's Setup: every villain starts set aside and the one brought in resolves its own Setup and When Revealed once
* A double-sided encounter card can flip and reveal its new face: its When Revealed resolves and a side scheme face gets its starting threat on top of what the card held
* An encounter-set ally the first player controls passes to the next first player when its controller is eliminated, instead of being discarded
* An ally whose base THW and ATK come from its controller's hero, and the loss when a required card leaves play now also fires when its controller is eliminated
* A trigger for a status card being placed on a character, announced by every path that gives one (effects, costs, toughness, re-given statuses)
* Main scheme decks can remove random alternative stages and shuffle one stage number's group (wave 7 §3.28)
* Damage rules that depend on the attacker or the attack: cannot take damage except from an attacker or attack card with a trait or a ranged attack, damage reduced per attack except for an attacker with a trait, and attacks that ignore retaliate
* A value for the number of cards of the most common type in a player's hand, counting the six player card types
* A way to name the player an enemy's attack is against, whoever defends it
* An effect that has a player choose a card type, with later parts of the ability reading the chosen type; the choice mechanism is a general choose-from-a-list
* A villain held at zero hit points by a card is defeated at once, with no defeating player, when that protection ends; triggers for a scheme's last threat being removed and for a named villain stage's defeat
* An attachment's conditional attach instruction as an ability that resolves when the card enters play, not stopped by canceling its When Revealed
* A rule on one card that makes other cards enter play exhausted, applied at the one point every card enters play
* A per-phase record of which characters attacked and thwarted, and a condition over it
* The victory display as a place effects can move cards to and bring them back from; a side scheme returning from it enters with its starting threat, unrevealed
* A constant ability that applies while its card is in the victory display, for the card's owner
* A permanent side scheme at zero threat is not defeated; a side scheme returning from the victory display gets its hinder
* A scheme can say only one named character may remove threat from it; supports, non-character encounter cards and removals with no player behind them still can
* A trigger for a card discarded from the top of a player's deck, heard from the discard pile; a card its response takes away is not counted by the ability that discarded it
* A rule that counts a printed resource icon more than once on cards discarded from the top of a player's deck; a card discarded as a cost now carries its icons to the effect
* A trigger filter for an attack that has a keyword, and 'Max 1 per attack' for events played from hand: a copy at the maximum is no longer offered or paid for
* A player side scheme nobody controls: thwartable by anyone, kept through eliminations, flipped to an environment when defeated; a crisis icon now stops threat removal sourced from such a card
* Campaigns: a retried scenario reuses a recorded choice, and a query for the damage on a card at the end of a game
* A card query for a printed cost of N or more, read after per player scaling
* A minion whose own When Defeated attaches it to an ally: it stays a minion, keeps its damage, is never defeated again, never activates and leaves with its host
* A rule that stops a card being flipped, a flip as an ability's cost, and the restricted limit checked after a flip without ever discarding a permanent card
* A player's attack can be redirected onto a friendly character, keeping its damage and keywords; a query for a card printing a named resource type, with wild as its own type or as any type
* An identity with two hero faces can be told which one to change to; a plain form change from a hero face of a three-face identity asks which other face
* A card query for whether a character has room for a stunned, confused or tough status card, sharing the decision the engine makes when it gives one
* A scheme icon printed on one face of an identity counts only while that face is showing (Archangel's acceleration icon)
* A rule under which an attack's boost cards are ignored: turned up and discarded, adding nothing and resolving no Boost ability, without being canceled
* An effect can send a played event back to its owner's hand once it has resolved, instead of to the discard pile
* Linked cards are set aside at setup for each deck that names them, owned by nobody until a player takes control (RRG p. 27)
* An encounter set can be included by a setup condition: the Dreadpool set joins the game when a player chooses the 'Pool aspect
* Card scripts can read the acceleration tokens on a card and the encounter icons in play
* Resource cards can generate a computed yield, a cost can be a chosen amount of damage to yourself (0 allowed), and a cost can spend a computed number of resources
* A card can count as more than one restricted card (Laser Swords), and a computed resource cost is priced correctly when an event is played as a response
* A computed resource cost can read the attack that triggered it, and overkill excess from an attacked ally is dealt to its controller
* A card effect can search the collection for a card and add a new copy to the game (Armed to the Teeth)
* Facts from outside the game (previous game result, minutes away, talked this phase) enter as setup input or a recorded answer, never read by the engine itself
* A trigger can answer either of two events, and "you" on an attachment or obligation no player controls means the player it is attached to (RRG pp. 8, 30)
* A basic-power bonus now applies to a basic recovery (Probability Field)
* An upgrade put into play faceup is attached as a play would attach it; a standing condition can apply as its card enters play; a thwart records the schemes it thwarted
* E.V.A. is discarded immediately when Fantomex is not in play, before any response to her entering play (NeXt Evolution FAQ)
* A player may trigger an Action ability, or play an Action event, during another player's turn (RRG Player Turn); basic powers, form changes and playing other cards stay own-turn only
* Card scripts can tell indirect damage and which basic power is in use; a stat bonus equal to another stat no longer overflows; a status card can only go on a character in play
* Target validity now judges an attack effect's damage: an enemy that cannot take it is not offered, and the ability cannot be initiated when no enemy can (RRG 1.8 Target, p. 43); a stunned character may still use an attack ability with no valid target (p. 41)
* A campaign scenario can name the modular sets it requires (CampaignNode.requiredModularSetIds): the game start hands them to the builder, campaignModularSetIds adds them to the picked sets, and createGame refuses a campaign game without one; NeXt Evolution's Juggernaut requires Black Tom Cassidy (MC40 p. 14)
* Restricted is a state limit: a restricted card can be played or put into play over the limit, and its controller then chooses which restricted cards to discard down to the limit (RRG p. 38); Armed to the Teeth's swap and a change of control are checked the same way
* A move of threat needs a source its threat can leave (RRG p. 30, owner ruling 2026-10-06): an ability whose moveThreat has no such scheme (a crisis icon, a threat-cannot-be-removed rule, no threat) is not offered and is refused before any cost, with its own reason; a chosen source offers only such schemes; new canRemoveThreatFrom predicate
* A "would" interrupt resolves before the same event's other interrupts are gathered (RRG 1.8 "Would", p. 48; owner ruling 2026-10-06): no ordering prompt between Hope's Captor and a Marauder's "attacks you" interrupt, and a replaced attack or scheme never opens the later tier. Opted in: every scripted "would attack" and "would scheme" replacement
* 0 damage opens no damage window (owner ruling 2026-10-06, RRG p. 14 "any amount of damage"): damage whose amount is 0 as it would be dealt, or that an interrupt prevented in full, has no "would take damage" window, no "after ... takes / is dealt damage" responses and sets off no "each time" effect; a tough status card is kept; the attack itself still resolves ("after attacks", "after defends", retaliate)
* A "(defense)" ability used after the Declare Defender step was declined makes the hero the defender with no DEF subtracted and no second prompt, and the attack no longer reads as undefended afterward (owner ruling 2026-10-06, RRG pp. 14-15); 18 tests pin each ordering
* An attack's response reads its target's status cards as the attack damaged it (owner ruling 2026-10-06): Cypher draws on a killing blow against a confused enemy
* An effect that instructs the play of an event with an Action ability may play it outside a player's turn (playFromHand.ignoreActionTiming; owner ruling 2026-10-06): Warpath plays a Hero Action event in the villain phase
* "You" in a constant ability is read through one resolver by every rule reader: on an obligation, an attachment on a player card or an engaged minion it names that player in every rule's queries, stat modifiers, keyword and trait grants; a boost card's own text (I See You's +1 boost icon) reads the player the activation resolves against
* "Would be defeated" replacements resolve in the earlier "would" interrupt tier (owner ruling 2026-10-06, RRG 1.8 p. 48): they resolve before any "is defeated" interrupt with no ordering prompt, and a replaced defeat never reaches Spider-Tracer, Priority Target and the like; 16 abilities marked, and MaGog, Collector and Odin's Torment now declare their replacement so the later tier stays closed
* Defending is one player's per enemy attack (RRG 1.8 pp. 14-15): once a player resolves a (defense) ability or has a character defending, other players' (defense) cards are not offered and their characters cannot be declared, with a readable reason; a (defense) ability followed by a basic defense by the same hero is one defense, announced once
* Pinned with tests that a card being played is never offered as a payer for itself, for a play an effect makes in the villain phase (Warpath), one on the player's turn, and an ordinary play; a second copy of it in hand is a payer like any other card
* Setup step 11 searches the encounter set-aside area as RRG 1.8 p. 51 reads (Q20 = B): a set-aside setup-keyword card begins in play unless the scenario's own text keeps it aside (setAsideUntilCalled: Mister Sinister's Superpowers, the Milano at Escape the Museum, The Hood's set-aside sets) or it is a campaign's supply copy (the Norn Stone); the Milano now enters by its keyword at Brotherhood of Badoon, Nebula and Ronan, with the same board
* "After X deals damage" fires after a tough status card and after a prevention alike (owner ruling 2026-10-07, RRG pp. 35, 44): a damage event carries the amount dealt and the amount taken, responses read one or the other (on.damage's dealt and taken, enforced by the validator), and Power Stone, Challenge Accepted and Schadenfreude read damage dealt; every damage trigger is classified in docs/dealt-vs-taken-audit.md; 0 damage as dealt still opens no window
