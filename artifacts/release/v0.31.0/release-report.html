+ the Trophy Room

New in this release
312 to earn · 9 ranks

Everything you do now counts. Wins, villains, campaigns, every hero you play, and a few things you'd rather forget earn achievements, and their points climb you from Sidekick in Training to Ultimate Champion.

312
Achievements in nine groups

Every hero gets one named for their line: "Another!", "I Am Iron Man", "Wakanda Forever!".

9
Ranks, shown on the Title

A rank badge with your points and the bar to the next rank, a Rank up banner and its own sound.

3
Popup settings

How long it stays, where it shows (title bar, top, middle, bottom) and its size. Several at once stack into one you tap open.

9
Silly packs

The funny achievements pay in silly packs instead of points, and achievements open new Extras.

The Fear No Evil
campaign

Daredevil's box · 6 scenarios
Kingpin waits at the end

Five scenarios you can play in any order against the villain the campaign gives you, then the Kingpin finale. Two are drawn before each issue, and the ones you leave behind keep progressing.

5×5
Schemes and villains, mixed

Hammerhead, Bullseye, Electro, Purple Man or Typhoid Mary against any of the five schemes. A first playthrough can follow the recommended order, or go random.

38
Comic pages

A five-page story for each scheme, built around the villain you face, and the Final Showdown.

8
Outcome scenes

Game Over shows that villain winning or going down. Win the finale with Daredevil seated for a different ending.

4
New heroes

Daredevil and Echo from the box, Jessica Jones and Luke Cage from their packs, each with a starter deck.
