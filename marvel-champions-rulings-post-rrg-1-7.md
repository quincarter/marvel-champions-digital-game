## December, 2025

### December 17, 2025 - Ruling 1

> **Questions:**
> 1. Can actions on minions be triggered by players who haven't engaged with them?
> 2. Does Aerial Evacuation reduce the damage dealt or the damage taken?
> 3. When Beguiled is attached to an ally that I control, will there be a process where the ally leaves play and a minion with the same title enters play? Or are no characters entering or leaving play?

**Official Ruling:**
1. **Yes.** As stated under "Ability" on page 4 of the online Rules Reference, any player can trigger an "Action" ability on an encounter card, with the exception of certain attachments and obligations.
2. Aerial Evacuation reduces the **damage taken**, as is the case for damage prevention abilities.
3. **No**, when Beguiled attaches to an ally, the ally does not leave play and the "minion" does not enter play; the character remains in play and retains all tokens and attachments. (The process is essentially a status change.)

*— Alex Werner, FFG Game Rules Specialist (December 17, 2025)*

---

### December 17, 2025 - Ruling 2

> **Questions:**
> *Context:* Seeking to understand how Basic Powers are used or not used when making their respective abilities:
> 1. The villain attacks me and I decide to NOT basic defend and take the attack undefended. On step 4 of Attack (Enemy Activation) [RRG v1.6 pg 8], I decide to use [Side Step](https://marvelcdb.com/card/14015) which now makes me the defender via Labeled Ability [RRG v1.6 pg 15; Defend]. Am I allowed to play [Stop Hitting Yourself](https://marvelcdb.com/card/52016)? If so, what would be the value (assuming my Hero has 2 DEF)?
> 2. Using Question 1's scenario: Imagine I also play [Not Today!](https://marvelcdb.com/card/38016) on top of Side Step (+2 DEF). Am I allowed to play Stop Hitting Yourself? If so, what is the value?
> 3. Building upon Questions 1 and 2: If I am playing against Enemy Leader Captain America who has [Cap's Shield](https://marvelcdb.com/card/56143), if I use an attack-labeled ability, am I considered to have "used my ATK to attack and damage"?

**Official Ruling:**
1. **Yes**, you would still be able to play "Stop Hitting Yourself" and deal damage based on your current DEF (2). Your hero still has a DEF value even if it wasn't used to reduce damage dealt.
2. **Yes**, similar to above, you would still be able to deal damage based on your DEF. *Not Today!* gives you +2 DEF, for a total of 4.
3. This depends on the attack-labeled ability being used. Many attack-labeled abilities do not use your ATK to deal damage, but some do:
   - If the ability is phrased as a flat damage amount (e.g., *"Deal # damage to an enemy"*), that would **not** allow you to take Cap's Shield.
   - If an ability calls out your ATK in some way (e.g., *"Deal damage to an enemy equal to your ATK"*), that **would** allow you to take Cap's Shield.

*— Alex Werner (December 17, 2025)*

### December 17, 2025 - Ruling 3

> **Questions:**
> *Context:* Clarifying the phrase "after the villain attacks you" and its meaning.
> 1. What does the phrase "After the villain attacks you" refer to—the character or the player? (Per RRG v1.6 pg 8, 10, 44).
> 2. Are there differences when text originates from a player card vs encounter card? E.g., Drax's [Payback](https://marvelcdb.com/card/19007) and [Concussive Bombs](https://marvelcdb.com/card/27137) have the exact same triggering condition. Can both be triggered when an ally controlled by Drax defends?
> 3. Should [Nick Fury's Stealth Suit](https://marvelcdb.com/card/50035a) and [Flash Freeze](https://marvelcdb.com/card/36012) be considered player-based or character-based?

**Official Ruling:**
* The general rule for abilities referencing "you" is that "you" refers to the player's identity where able, and otherwise refers to that player; with the exception being **"After [enemy] attacks you"** abilities where **"you" refers to the player**, because such abilities also trigger when an ally that player controls is attacked.
* Drax's *Payback* and *Concussive Bombs* are both phrased using the "you" exception in the Rules Reference, so **both can be triggered when Drax's ally is attacked**.
* Nick Fury's *Stealth Suit* triggers "when" an enemy would attack, and Storm's *Flash Freeze* triggers "when" the villain attacks, neither of which fall into the "you" exception. Stealth Suit only triggers when Nick Fury would be attacked, and Flash Freeze is only playable when Storm is attacked.

*— Alex Werner (December 17, 2025)*

### December 17, 2025 - Ruling 4

> **Questions:**
> *Context:* "Removed from the game" vs "outside the current game" states.
> 1. If a card is removed from the game, is it now part of your collection? Can you bring that physical card back into the game via [Armed to the Teeth](https://marvelcdb.com/card/44009)?
> 2. In older rulings, [Med Lab](https://marvelcdb.com/card/38028) could retrieve an ally from out-of-play areas if defeated by consequential damage. Is this still correct? If [Odin](https://marvelcdb.com/card/21139a) [KING side] is defeated by consequential damage and removed from the game, can Med Lab retrieve Odin?
> 3. Is there a difference between cards "in the game, but out of play" (e.g., set-aside) compared to "outside the current game" (e.g., collection)? Can FIND only find cards within the game?

**Official Ruling:**
1. Once a card is removed from the game, it **cannot** be returned to the game by any means (e.g., *Armed to the Teeth*), and it does not become a part of the collection.
2. Med Lab can target allies from out-of-play areas that are still in the game, such as a player's discard pile. It **cannot** target allies that have been removed from the game.
   - Because Odin is removed from the game via a Forced Interrupt, he is removed before Med Lab's Response can trigger, making him untargetable.
3. **Yes**, there is a distinct difference between "in the game, out of play" and "outside the current game." The **Find** keyword can only search "in game" areas.

*— Alex Werner (December 17, 2025)*

## January, 2026

### January 11, 2026 - Ruling 1

> **Questions:**
> 1. When exactly is the Fearless Determination side scheme discarded after it's defeated? (Amplify icon)
> 2. Does Teddy Altman's ability prevent playing a second shape shift upgrade (or discard down to 1)?
> 3. If I defeat an enemy with the first sentence of Armored Defense, does the second sentence still resolve and give Hulkling a tough status card?
> 4. With Cap's Shield: If I attack a minion with my hero using my ATK and that attack has overkill and damages Captain America, does that trigger the response on Cap's Shield?
> 5. With Captain Marvel Leader's forced response: If her attack against an ally has overkill and defeats a hero that way, does she trigger twice and gain 2 energy counters?
> 6. Can I count the number of cards left in the encounter deck or player deck to get that information (without looking at faces)? What about with eyes closed?
> 7. Can I use modular sets from one side with a Leader from the other side in co-op mode?

**Official Ruling:**
* When Fearless Determination is defeated, it is considered in play until its When Defeated ability resolves. Its amplify icon remains in effect when Captain America activates.
* Teddy Altman's constant ability prevents playing another Shapeshift upgrade while already having one in play (cannot play a second and discard down).
* **Yes**, if Armored Defense defeats the attacking minion, Hulkling still receives a Tough status card.
* **Yes**, if you use your ATK (e.g., basic attack) and deal damage to Captain America via overkill, you trigger the Forced Response on Cap's Shield.
* **Yes**, if Captain Marvel attacks with overkill and defeats both an ally and identity, her Forced Response triggers twice to place 2 total energy counters.
* **Yes**, you can count the total number of cards left in a deck, as long as you do not change their order or inspect facedown cards.
* **No**, you cannot use Registration modular sets with a Resistance leader, nor vice versa.

*— Alex Werner (January 11, 2026)*

### January 11, 2026 - Ruling 2

> **Questions:**
> Clarification on the triggering conditions "attacks and damages" and "attacks and defeats" with the Overkill keyword:
> 1. Can you steal Cap's Shield from overkill damage since you only damaged him but didn't target/attack him?
> 2. Can the Captain Marvel leader get an energy counter from defeating an identity with overkill?
> 3. If Jigsaw attacks with overkill and damages both an ally and identity, does his ability trigger twice and place 4 threat on Criminal Underworld?

**Official Ruling:**
* **Yes**, assuming the attacker used their ATK (e.g., basic attack) and that attack had overkill, damage dealt to the Captain America leader triggers the Forced Response on Cap's Shield and attaches it to the attacker.
* **Yes**, if Captain Marvel attacks with overkill and defeats both an ally and an identity, her Forced Response triggers for both instances, placing 2 total energy counters.
* **Yes**, if Jigsaw attacks with overkill and damages both an ally and an identity, his Forced Response triggers twice and places 4 total threat.

*— Alex Werner (January 11, 2026)*

### January 11, 2026 - Ruling 3

> **Question:**
> Regarding printed resources: Nova's Nemesis side scheme "Bring the War!" requires players to discard cards with a printed wild resource. Does Supernova Helmet count because of the printed resource in its ability text, or only cards with a printed resource in the bottom-left resource area?

**Official Ruling:**
**No.** For the When Revealed ability on "Bring the War!", the discarded card must have a wild resource icon printed in its bottom-left corner. The only time a "printed resource" can refer to a resource generated by an ability is when paying a cost.

### January 11, 2026 - Ruling 4

> **Question:**
> If I attack a guard minion with the Aggression Bombshell ally (Ironheart pack) while the only other enemy in play is the villain, how is her damage dividing ability resolved? Can damage be dealt to the villain? Is she considered to have attacked the villain?

**Official Ruling:**
You resolve Bombshell's ability by dividing her attack as evenly as possible among valid targets. In this scenario, the villain is not a valid target due to Guard, so the villain is ignored; all of her damage is dealt to the guard minion.

*— Alex Werner (January 11, 2026)*

### January 17, 2026 - Ruling 1

> **Questions:**
> 1. Protection ally Rogue copies the base ATK and THW of her target (instead of printed). Hope Summers has star icons as values, defined by her textbox as equal to your hero's ATK/THW. The Star Icon RRG entry says when referenced by an ability, it's treated as 0. What bonus does Rogue get?
> 2. The "Leaves Play" entry added a "when a card leaves play" bullet point. Is that timing before the card leaves play or simultaneous?

**Official Ruling:**
1. The star icon entry was outdated; the value of a star icon is defined by its associated ability (defaulting to 0 only when there is no associated ability). Because Hope Summers defines her star icons to match your hero's basic powers, Rogue copying Hope adds your hero's power values to her own.
2. The updated bullet under "Leaves Play" is **not an interrupt ability**; this bullet and all subbullets are carried out simultaneously with the card leaving play.

*— Alex Werner (January 17, 2026)*

### January 17, 2026 - Ruling 2

> **Question:**
> Winter Soldier vs Black Widow villain (Agents of S.H.I.E.L.D.): Black Widow attacks, Winter Soldier plays *Arm Block* (attack/defense event). Black Widow's interrupt triggers because Arm Block is an attack, revealing A.I.M. Grunt. What happens? Does A.I.M. Grunt take the damage and the damage cancellation fails, or does defense apply to BW while attack damages the Grunt?

**Official Ruling:**
Sequence of events:
1. Black Widow initiates her attack.
2. Winter Soldier plays *Arm Block* (attack/defense event).
3. Black Widow's interrupt triggers, revealing A.I.M. Grunt.
4. A.I.M. Grunt is now being attacked by Arm Block, taking the 3 damage.
5. Arm Block can then prevent damage from Black Widow's attack, because her attack triggered the "When an enemy attacks" condition on Arm Block, and "prevent all damage from that enemy's attack" refers to that initial attack made by Black Widow (since A.I.M. Grunt is not making an attack).

*— Alex Werner (January 17, 2026)*

### January 17, 2026 - Ruling 3

> **Questions:**
> 1. Under RRG 1.7 Piercing and prevent abilities: Some abilities prevent all damage without specifying "dealt" vs "taken". If an enemy with Piercing attacks Rogue using *Bulletproof Belle* (prevents all damage and grants Tough), does Piercing remove Tough?
> 2. Piercing discards Tough before dealing damage (calculated after ATK – DEF). Falcon's *Aerial Evacuation* interrupt triggers on "would deal". Does Piercing remove Tough before Aerial Evacuation prevents damage?

**Official Ruling:**
1. Effects that "prevent damage" prevent **damage taken, not dealt**. If Rogue plays Bulletproof Belle and gains a Tough status card, an attack with Piercing that still deals damage to her will remove that Tough status card.
2. Piercing triggers when the attack would deal damage (same window as Aerial Evacuation). However, **keywords have timing priority over triggered abilities**. Piercing removes the Tough status card before Aerial Evacuation triggers to prevent damage taken.

*— Alex Werner (January 17, 2026)*

### January 17, 2026 - Ruling 5

> **Questions:**
> In The Wrecking Crew scenario:
> 1. When playing a Cosmic Entity event, into which encounter deck is it shuffled?
> 2. Can I play Face the Past? If so, into which discard pile is the nemesis minion discarded?

*— Alex Werner (January 17, 2026)*

### January 26, 2026 - Ruling 1

> **Question:**
> In RRG 1.7, the target rule was adjusted to state: you cannot target a character that cannot take damage with an ability that **ONLY** deals damage to the target. Does this mean Nova ally Ms. Marvel (cost: deal 1 damage to her and exhaust her) can be used when *Go for Champions!* is active?

**Official Ruling:**
A cost in an ability is part of that ability and subject to valid target rules. Because Ms. Marvel's ability cost both deals damage **and** exhausts her, damage is not the *only* effect on her. She remains a valid target for her cost even when *Go for Champions!* prevents her from taking damage. *(Note: Go for Champions! now removes itself from the game, so she must return a different event to hand).*

*— Alex Werner (January 26, 2026)*

### January 26, 2026 - Ruling 2

> **Question:**
> If an all-purpose counter is placed on a card, does it gain the counter type defined by that card? E.g., via Cameo, Captain Americat places a toon counter on Drax—does it become a vengeance counter?

**Official Ruling:**
**Correct.** Once an all-purpose counter is placed on a card, it gains the type of counter defined on the card it occupies. A counter placed on Drax via Captain Americat becomes a vengeance counter.

*— Alex Werner (January 26, 2026)*

### January 26, 2026 - Ruling 3

> **Questions:**
> In RRG 1.7, Overkill was changed from "dealt" to "taken" damage, while "Excess Damage" remained defined as damage dealt beyond remaining hit points.
> - Does Rocket Raccoon's *"Murdered You!"* check excess damage dealt even if no excess damage was taken?
> - Does *Into the Fray* remove threat based on damage dealt or taken? What about with *Marked*?
> - Example: Rocket plays Into the Fray on Nimrod (4 HP remaining). Rocket deals 6, Nimrod takes 3. Does Rocket draw? Does Into the Fray remove threat?

**Official Ruling:**
* Overkill counts damage **taken**, whereas Excess Damage is damage **dealt** beyond remaining hit points.
* Excess damage can be dealt even if the target does not take that damage.
* Rocket Raccoon's *"Murdered You!"* triggers when he deals excess damage, regardless of damage taken.
* *Into the Fray* checks excess damage dealt: Rocket deals 6 damage to Nimrod (4 HP), so excess damage dealt is 2. Therefore, 2 threat is removed and Rocket draws 1 card.
* If Nimrod had *Marked* attached, Nimrod took only 3 damage (less than his 4 HP), so 0 excess damage was taken, resulting in no overkill damage dealt to the villain.

*— Alex Werner (January 26, 2026)*

**Yes.** Acute Tactility triggers upon the last threat being removed (Interrupt), which precedes the scheme being "defeated" (Forced Interrupt). It discards first, allowing Focus the Senses to retrieve it.

### August 3, 2026 - Ruling 6

> **Question:**
> Definition of "Exactly Defeat" vs damage reduction effects (Thumbelina, Kree Combat Armor): Can Thumbelina be "exactly defeated"?

**Official Ruling:**
To "exactly defeat" a character means defeating them by an attack that dealt **no excess damage**. Characters that reduce damage taken generally cannot be exactly defeated because excess damage must be dealt to overcome the reduction.

*— Alex Werner (August 3, 2026)*
