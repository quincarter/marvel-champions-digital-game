# Current status

[README](../../README.md) · [Current status](current-status.md) · [Architecture](architecture.md) · [Downloads](downloads.md) · [Contributing](../../CONTRIBUTING.md)

The game is playable end to end across **every released Marvel Champions product**: the Core Set through cycle 11,
Fear No Evil, and the Shadowland scenario pack. That is 64 packs, 69 heroes, 68 scenarios and 331 encounter sets.
Play alone (one person playing 1–4 heroes on one device) or **online with friends**, each on their own device. Elektra
and Iron Fist (the next two hero packs) are in the card pool and stay hidden until their official text is published.

Play it in the browser at [marvel.champions-digital-game.com](https://marvel.champions-digital-game.com), download
the Windows, macOS, Linux and Android apps, or sideload the iPhone and iPad app ([Downloads](downloads.md),
[iPhone and iPad install](ios-install.md)).

## What you can play

- **Every hero, scenario and encounter set from the Core Set through Shadowland**: the hero packs, campaign boxes and
  scenario packs of The Rise of Red Skull, The Galaxy's Most Wanted, The Mad Titan's Shadow, Sinister Motives, Mutant
  Genesis, NeXt Evolution, Age of Apocalypse, Agents of S.H.I.E.L.D., Civil War (with Synthezoid Smackdown), and Fear
  No Evil (with Jessica Jones, Luke Cage and Shadowland). Each card is scripted against its printed text and errata
  and covered by rules tests.
- **Online multiplayer** (new in v1.0.0), opt-in from Play online on the Title. Single player stays fully offline.
  - **Watch a live game** from a link or the Discord card's Watch live button, following every move with a LIVE marker.
  - **Rooms**: host a room or join one with a 6-character code or link, set your online name, and use the lobby (seats,
    host settings, chat). Each player picks their own hero and deck, custom decks included, and the host checks every
    deck at Start. Hosts choose Strict or Host control play, who may Undo, and whether to list the room in Live now.
  - **Play together**: seated players play their heroes from their own devices, with a whose-turn line, requests to the
    host, take-over or hand-off of an absent player's seat, in-game chat, and Show my hand for teaching games. A reload
    rejoins your room, and a guest whose host closes the room keeps the game as a single-player save.
  - **Trickster Takeover Epic Multiplayer**: several tables against one shared Loki, God of Lies, with a pod strip,
    cross-table attacks and thwarts on Mangog and the Door Between Worlds, and watchers switching tables.
  - **Civil War Competitive**: two teams (1v1 or 2v2), each sealing a scenario for the other, leader against leader,
    with Choosing Sides and a tie-break result sheet. Competitive games count as played only.
  - **Multiplayer achievements**: 49 achievements for playing together, Epic, Competitive, watching and hosting.
- **Campaign mode for all eleven campaigns**: The Rise of Red Skull, Galaxy's Most Wanted, The Mad Titan's Shadow,
  Sinister Motives, Mutant Genesis, MojoMania, NeXt Evolution, Age of Apocalypse, Agents of S.H.I.E.L.D., Fear No Evil
  and Shadowland. The campaign log carries between scenarios, with expert campaign rules, story beats and each box's
  own between-game choices. Custom (Mod Store) heroes and decks can join a campaign.
- **Standard, expert, Heroic and Skirmish**: set a heroic level or play one villain version, on Table setup and for
  each campaign issue.
- **Civil War's custom scenario builder**: pick a leader, two main scheme stages and three or four modular sets, or let
  the game deal a random (or random and blind) scenario.
- **The Mod Store**: browse, install, update and remove reviewed community packs (heroes, villains, scenarios,
  campaigns and house rules) with their own trophies, on every platform. Packs are made with the mod SDK; see
  [Mods](../../README.md#mods).
- **Decks**: every hero's starter deck, decks built in the in-app deck builder, and decks imported from MarvelCDB, all
  checked against the Rules Reference's deckbuilding rules. Community decklists from MarvelCDB are on the Decks screen,
  and the deck builder suggests cards from your own games.
- **Replays and Past games**: watch any finished game back with play, pause, step and a seek bar, share it as a link
  or export it, and search every finished game from Past games.
- **Your games, kept**: Your stats (record, streaks, win rates and loss causes per villain, modular set and hero, with
  opt-in anonymous global win rates), a Trophy Room of achievements, and official promo cards earned by winning.
- **Extras**: story comics, side stories, the rulebooks, hero and villain galleries, artwork, promo cards and a music
  jukebox, unlocked as you play.
- **Guided mode** with Try-it lessons for new players, **Undo** (as far back as you allow in Settings), **Manual
  adjust** under Cheats to fix the table by hand, and **local save and resume**. Adjusted games earn no stats.
- **Your saves on every device**: send saves to another phone, tablet or computer by code or QR, export them, and keep
  saved games, decks, campaigns and unlocks in sync across devices with no login.
- **English and Spanish**: Spanish card scans and text, Spanish builds of every release, and the menus and release
  notes in Spanish, with more of the game following.
- **Desktop, tablet, phone, keyboard and gamepad**: one client that runs in the browser and ships as Windows, macOS,
  Linux, Android and iOS apps, with the card hover popover on desktop, a thumb-reach layout on phones, full controller
  support with button glyphs, and dark mode. Desktop apps update themselves from the update banner.
