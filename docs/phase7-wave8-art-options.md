# Wave 8 art options (Age of Apocalypse)

Candidates for the six open art slots, gathered 2026-10-07 for the owner to choose from. Nothing has been downloaded.
Every option is a file on Marvel Database (marvel.fandom.com); sizes come from the wiki's own API. Each image was
looked at as a small thumbnail only, so a signature or corner mark may have been missed, and artist names are from
memory, not checked. Only Marvel Database was searched: larger or cleaner copies may exist elsewhere. All are portrait
unless stated. Open a page link to see the picture.

Where the picked file goes: `art/scenarios/_pending/<id>/villain.<ext>` for slots 1 to 5 (ids `unus`,
`four-horsemen`, `apocalypse`, `dark-beast`, `en-sabah-nur`), `art/campaigns/aoa/cover.<ext>` for slot 6.

## 1. Unus

No good solo Age of Apocalypse art of him turned up; 1A gives up the era, 1B gives up quality.

- **1A (top pick).** X-Men '97 Strike Files Infinity Comic #3: Unus on a ledge in his classic red costume, hand
  glowing. No text. 800×1280. Animated-series styling, not the armored Age of Apocalypse look.
  [page](<https://marvel.fandom.com/wiki/File:Gunther_Bain_(Earth-92131)_from_X-Men_%2797_Strike_Files_Infinity_Comic_Vol_1_3.jpg>)
- **1B.** X-Men Alpha #1 (1995) panel: the real Age of Apocalypse Unus in armor leading Infinites. 644×840. Low
  resolution, two speech bubbles.
  [page](<https://marvel.fandom.com/wiki/File:Gunther_Bain_(Earth-295)_from_X-Men_Alpha_Vol_1_1_001.jpg>)
- **1C.** X-Men: The Early Years #8 cover: a large Unus in front of the original X-Men. 1848×2772. Has the logo and
  trade dress. [page](https://marvel.fandom.com/wiki/File:X-Men_The_Early_Years_Vol_1_8.jpg)
- **1D.** Instead of Unus: Astonishing X-Men #3 (1995) cover, Rogue fighting Infinite soldiers. 1041×1600. Has the
  logo; a hero is the focus. [page](https://marvel.fandom.com/wiki/File:Astonishing_X-Men_Vol_1_3.jpg)

## 2. The Four Horsemen

- **2A (top pick).** Extraordinary X-Men #12 textless (2016): Apocalypse towering over four Horsemen. 779×1200.
  Apocalypse is in it, and these Horsemen are the Colossus / Venom / Deadpool / Moon Knight versions. The same art at
  2245×3338 carries an "Apocalypse Wars" title.
  [page](https://marvel.fandom.com/wiki/File:Extraordinary_X-Men_Vol_1_12_Textless.jpg)
- **2B.** Extraordinary X-Men #8 splash: the same four, close up, no Apocalypse. 1800×2523. One small speech bubble.
  [page](<https://marvel.fandom.com/wiki/File:Horsemen_of_Apocalypse_(Earth-16558)_from_Extraordinary_X-Men_Vol_1_8_001.jpg>)
- **2C.** X of Swords: Stasis #1 textless (2020): the original Horsemen among a line of champions before a gate.
  1988×3056. More than four figures; nobody on a horse.
  [page](https://marvel.fandom.com/wiki/File:X_of_Swords_Stasis_Vol_1_1_Textless.jpg)
- **2D.** Heralds of Apocalypse #1 (2023): the original four in armor. 1665×2560. Has the logo.
  [page](https://marvel.fandom.com/wiki/File:X-Men_Before_The_Fall_-_Heralds_of_Apocalypse_Vol_1_1.jpg)
- **2E.** X-Factor #24 (1987): War, Famine and Pestilence on flying steeds with Archangel as Death. 1651×2223. A
  caption box and a panel border; 1980s flat color.
  [page](<https://marvel.fandom.com/wiki/File:Horsemen_of_Apocalypse_(Earth-616)_from_X-Factor_Vol_1_24_002.jpg>)

## 3. Apocalypse

- **3A (top pick for the look).** Tomb of Apocalypse #1 Villalobos virgin variant: full figure, blue light, glowing
  eye. 1821×2800. Not an Age of Apocalypse book; artist's signature bottom left.
  [page](https://marvel.fandom.com/wiki/File:Tomb_of_Apocalypse_Vol_1_1_Villalobos_Virgin_Variant.jpg)
- **3B (top pick for the era).** X-Men Alpha #1 (1995) full page: Apocalypse in his citadel. 1073×1650. Caption boxes
  and speech bubbles along the top and right edge (a crop to the figure loses most).
  [page](<https://marvel.fandom.com/wiki/File:En_Sabah_Nur_(Earth-295)_from_X-Men_Alpha_Vol_1_1_0001.jpg>)
- **3C.** Age of Apocalypse (2015) #1 promo variant textless: his face over Magneto's X-Men. 688×1044. Low resolution;
  heroes in front. [page](https://marvel.fandom.com/wiki/File:Age_of_Apocalypse_Vol_2_1_Promo_Variant_Textless.jpg)
- **3D.** Uncanny X-Men (2016) #6 classic variant textless: a roaring Apocalypse head behind Psylocke and Archangel.
  964×1500. [page](https://marvel.fandom.com/wiki/File:Uncanny_X-Men_Vol_4_6_Classic_Variant_Textless.jpg)
- **3E.** X-Men of Apocalypse Alpha #1 Clarke variant (2025): Apocalypse in a burning citadel. 1821×2800. Large logo.
  [page](https://marvel.fandom.com/wiki/File:X-Men_of_Apocalypse_Alpha_Vol_1_1_Clarke_Variant.jpg)

## 4. Dark Beast

Nothing textless above 843 px wide was found, and no lab scene without speech bubbles.

- **4A (top pick).** X-Men (1991) #201 pin-up: Dark Beast in a long coat on a moonlit balcony, grinning. No text.
  843×1477. [page](<https://marvel.fandom.com/wiki/File:Henry_McCoy_(Earth-295)_from_X-Men_Vol_2_201_001.jpg>)
- **4B.** X-Man #9 cover (1995): Dark Beast with Nate Grey over his shoulder. 1280×1968. Has the logo. Would also
  serve as his "villain wins" picture. [page](https://marvel.fandom.com/wiki/File:X-Man_Vol_1_9.jpg)
- **4C.** Marvel Onslaught Ultra trading card (1996): a painted close-up of his grin. 1132×1600. A caption strip
  across the bottom (croppable).
  [page](<https://marvel.fandom.com/wiki/File:Henry_McCoy_(Earth-295)_from_X-Men_(Trading_Cards)_Marvel_Onslaught_Ultra_(1996).jpg>)
- **4D.** Marvel Legends Age of Apocalypse package art: Dark Beast clawing forward, Apocalypse behind. 678×1000. Low
  resolution; toy packaging art.
  [page](<https://marvel.fandom.com/wiki/File:Henry_McCoy_(Earth-295)_from_Marvel_Legends_Age_of_Apocalypse_Final_Dark_Beast_Package_Art.jpg>)

  [page](https://marvel.fandom.com/wiki/File:Immortal_X-Men_Vol_1_16_Textless.jpeg)
- **5B.** X-Men Red (2022) #16 splash: Apocalypse in ancient armor striding out of light. 1550×2388. One small speech
  bubble and a small mark.
  [page](<https://marvel.fandom.com/wiki/File:En_Sabah_Nur_(Earth-616)_from_X-Men_Red_Vol_2_16_001.jpg>)
- **5C.** Rise of Apocalypse #3 or #4 covers (1996): the ancient-Egypt origin. About 1990×3056. Large logo and cover
  text. [#3](https://marvel.fandom.com/wiki/File:Rise_of_Apocalypse_Vol_1_3.jpg) ·
  [#4](https://marvel.fandom.com/wiki/File:Rise_of_Apocalypse_Vol_1_4.jpg)
- **5D.** X-Men (2019) #13 Asrar variant textless: Apocalypse with a scimitar before a pyramid. 875×1330 (the larger
  copy has the logo). [page](https://marvel.fandom.com/wiki/File:X-Men_Vol_5_13_Asrar_Variant_Textless.jpg)
