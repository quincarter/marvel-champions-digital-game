## Every card gets its own row

The card lists were hiding cards: any two with the same name and type shared one row, so only one of the seven Spider-Man allies ever showed (#598). Every card has its own row now, and search reads subtitles and finds "spdr" (#596, #597). A deck's own list sorts by aspect, type, cost or name, and the deck builder has a Review button (#594, #595). SP//dr gets her missing scans, her payment tiles and a quieter empty hand (#599, #600, #601), and 23 more card faces show in Spanish. All eight reports came from @bluefireF5ran. Thank you!

**[Read the full v0.42.0 report on the site ↗](https://marvel-champions-digital-game.netlify.app/releases/v0.42.0/)**: every card in its own row, sorting and reviewing a deck, the SP//dr fixes, the Spanish scans, and the thank-you wall.
