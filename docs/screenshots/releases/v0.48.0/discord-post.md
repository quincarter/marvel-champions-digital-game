**Marvel Champions: Digital Edition v0.48.0: With Great Reports Comes Great Patching** 🃏

bluefireF5ran sent eighteen ideas, and every one of them is in this release.

🎯 **Clearer table**: the main scheme shows "+N per round", stats show their printed star, an attachment says what removing it costs, and a deck about to run out turns red.
🗂️ **Play area order**: Settings ▸ Cards sorts your play area as played, by type, by cost or by name.
🌊 **Surge marks**: the log and the villain phase recap tag the card that surged and the card it brought.
📚 **Faster pickers**: fold each cycle's shelf, roll a Random box, scenario or villain, and pick which deck list opens first.
➕ **Extra modular sets**: a house rule, off by default, for more sets than a scenario asks for.
🃏 **Every face**: the deck check shows every identity face and both sides of double-sided cards.
📊 **Your decks**: a Decks tab in Your stats for every seat, and Past games exports to MC Tracker as a CSV.
💬 **Discord button** on the title menu.
🛠️ **Fixes**: Clear the Area draws with Shrink (thanks, sgaon42!), Daredevil's Sense deck has a Play it button (thanks, javgoro!), and Mod Store scenarios get Standard II/III, Expert II and Skirmish (thanks, ZackBurnley!).

Downloads: https://github.com/quincarter/marvel-champions-digital-game/releases/tag/v0.48.0

Thank you, bluefireF5ran, and everyone who played and reported!

**Marvel Champions: Digital Edition v0.48.0: grandes reportes conllevan grandes parches** 🃏

🃏 **Cada cara**: la revisión del mazo muestra cada cara de la identidad y las dos caras de las cartas de doble cara.
📊 **Tus mazos**: una pestaña Decks en Your stats para cada asiento, y Past games exporta a MC Tracker en CSV.
