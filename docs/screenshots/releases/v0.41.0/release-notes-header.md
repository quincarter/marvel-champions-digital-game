## Win the game. Keep the art.

Winning now earns you the official promo cards: 128 scans of 25 heroes, 12 villains and villain teams, and 5 events, opened by 3 or 10 wins and picked in Extras to replace a card's normal art, with the wins already in your save counted (#192, asked for by @PermaKarma). Take Your Seats can show Game art, Card art or Promo art on its hero tiles. Every release now has a home at /releases/, opened from the Title's Release notes link (#588). Settings has a search that finds any setting and anything on the pages under it, typos included (#576). Install for Steam is one script for each system at a link that never changes, and the game updates through it from inside, Game Mode included (#582). If you installed with an older script, run the new one once. Cards left lists the villain's stages, and a setting changed from Pause reaches the table right away (#575). The You / Everyone switch is easier to find, and you can start sharing from Your stats (#563). The Once and Future Kang and Escaped Convict now play as printed. 21 cards that showed the wrong scan show their own, and Taskmaster keeps his face on the stage-flip splash. A controller reaches the Title's theme and language buttons (#579), rank bars are red in light and dark mode (#587), and device sync keeps up with a long game (#585). Thanks to @PermaKarma (#569, #571, #572) for Team-Building Exercise's payment, telling Field Agents apart and Wounded Hela's missing card, and to @Vasko47 (#568) for Cloak of Levitation under Unnatural Storm.
