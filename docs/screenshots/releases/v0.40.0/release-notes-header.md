## Every game counts now.

Your stats is here: a dashboard of every game you've finished, opened from the Title, Decks & Collection, the Trophy Room, Settings or Pause, with your record and streaks, how you lose, your favorite heroes and toughest villains, and the cards you actually play (#529). Each villain, modular set and hero now has a win rate and a bar of why those games were lost, and if you opt in you can see everyone's too (#542, suggested by @neopolii on Discord). The deck builder puts all of it to work: marvelcdb-style search terms with a ? cheat sheet, quick filter chips, a sort that holds while you search, standard deck rules in Settings, draw odds, and cards suggested from your own winning decks against each villain (#531). In a game, tap your deck to see what's still in it (#540, also from @neopolii), and Inspect steps a hero through Hero, Alter-Ego and every form, asked for by @andy-xxvii27 (#532). Part 6 of the rules audit by @bluefireF5ran closes out the series with seven rulings (#498). Thanks to @zackburnley (#514, #515, #517, #518, #521, #522) for the reports: the top-bar buttons stay off popups, Bloody Mary's armor breaks at 5 in standard, the loss screen names the right scheme, and Protection Racket lets you pick your main scheme. On Android, Export save data opens the Save dialog and the keyboard lays over the game.
