**Marvel Champions: Digital Edition v0.45.0: The Mod Store Is Open** 🛒

Community heroes and scenarios now install from inside the game.

🛒 **Mod Store (Beta)**: open Extras ▸ Mod Store (Beta) to browse fan-made packs, see their cards, and install, update or remove them. Eight packs to start: Carnage, Gwenpool, Night Thrasher and Yondu, the Mephisto and The Punisher scenarios, and the Lost Traits and GMW Fixes house rules. It's a beta: Report a problem is right there in the store.
🏆 **Mod achievements**: 15 new ones in the Trophy Room. They give no points, so mods never move your rank.
🎬 **Replays**: watch any game back on the board. Play, pause, step and seek at 1×, 2× or 4×, then export the replay or the log, or share a link. Find it on Game over and in Extras ▸ Open a replay.
🗂️ **Past games**: a new list beside Extras on the title screen keeps every game you've finished, won or lost and why. Pick one to watch, share or export it.
🏠 **A home of its own**: the game has its own website, https://champions-digital-game.com, with news, every hero and villain, the mods and downloads. The game itself has moved: play at https://marvel.champions-digital-game.com.
🛠️ **Fixes**: the Briefing no longer goes blank on some Android phones, and Stealth Training answers Secure the Landing Pad's defeat.
✨ **Smoother screens**: Title, Extras and the Mod Store fade in fully drawn, and Extras tabs switch without a hitch.
🥋 Elektra and Iron Fist are on the way.
