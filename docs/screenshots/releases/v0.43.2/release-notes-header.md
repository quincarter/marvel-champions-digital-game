## Crushing Blow Lands Again

Hulk's Crushing Blow can be played again when your hand holds other resources beside a [physical] one, thanks to @dalamillo's report (#648). @bluefireF5ran replayed real games from their saves and fixed three rules bugs: a second signature side scheme in each Wrecking Crew deck (#644), encounter cards that hit an alter-ego's hero (#645), and Assault on NORAD answering the threat that completed The Crimson Cowl (#646). Thank you!
