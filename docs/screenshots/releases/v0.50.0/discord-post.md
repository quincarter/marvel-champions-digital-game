# v0.50.0: Awesome Mix, Now Playing 🎧

Every playable scenario now has its own music.

🎵 **43 tracks of new music**: Kingpin, all three Shadowland scenarios, Breakout, Kang, Absorbing Man, Zola, and Civil War's Captain Marvel, She-Hulk and Vision get their own battle, victory and defeat songs. Fear No Evil and Shadowland get a campaign interlude and finale, and Stop the Presses! and Crossbones get new takes.
📻 **The jukebox**: 221 songs in Extras ▸ Music, each opened as you earn it.
⚡ **Updates that install themselves**: the desktop game now updates itself from any installer: Update downloads while you play, Restart applies it. It starts with the next release, so grab v0.50.0 from the website.
🎬 **Replays**: a shared replay link opens on its own loading screen, then a versus screen of the villain against your heroes.
🚂 **Add to Steam**: Settings ▸ More ▸ Add to Steam puts the game in your Steam library with its art.
🎮 **Your mods on Discord**: a Mod Store pack now shows its own villain and hero on your Discord profile.
🔴 **Mod updates**: the Title's Mod Store button shows how many of your mods have an update, and on Linux mod downloads show their progress.
🦾 **Power Man's chi**: removing chi counters asks a clear question with buttons you can see.
🔗 **The website**: Get the game on your Discord profile opens the website's downloads, and on a computer the website offers the Steam script beside the download.

Thank you, Merman-Helville, for the Power Man report, and Patatasaurio, Neptune (Typhon00) and PermaKarma, whose packs the Discord fix was checked with!
