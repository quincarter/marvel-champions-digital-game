A small release with a big soundtrack.

🎵 **33 tracks of new music**: Kingpin, all three Shadowland scenarios, Breakout, Kang, Absorbing Man and Zola get their own battle, victory and defeat songs. Fear No Evil and Shadowland get a campaign interlude and finale.
🔁 **New takes**: Stop the Presses! and Crossbones get remade battle and defeat tracks, and Stop the Presses! gets its victory track, Extra! Extra!
📻 **The jukebox**: each song opens in Extras ▸ Music as you earn it in your games. That's 212 songs in all.
🎮 **Your mods on Discord**: in the desktop app, a Mod Store pack now shows its own villain and hero on your Discord profile instead of only the logo.
🔗 **Get the game**: the button on your Discord profile now opens the downloads on the website.
🚂 **Steam script**: on a computer, the website's top offers the Steam script right beside the download.

Thank you, Patatasaurio and Neptune (Typhon00), whose Gwenpool and Mephisto packs the Discord fix was checked with, and every Mod Store creator!
