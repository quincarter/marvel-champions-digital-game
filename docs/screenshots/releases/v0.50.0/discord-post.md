# v0.50.0: Awesome Mix, Now Playing 🎧

Every playable scenario now has its own music.

🎵 **43 tracks of new music**: Kingpin, all three Shadowland scenarios, Breakout, Kang, Absorbing Man, Zola, and Civil War's Captain Marvel, She-Hulk and Vision get their own battle, victory and defeat songs. Fear No Evil and Shadowland get a campaign interlude and finale.
🔁 **New takes**: Stop the Presses! and Crossbones get remade tracks, and Stop the Presses! gets its victory track, Extra! Extra!
📻 **The jukebox**: each song opens in Extras ▸ Music as you earn it in your games. That's 221 songs in all.
⚡ **Updates that install themselves**: the desktop game now updates from inside the game, whichever installer you used. Update downloads it while you play, Restart puts you on it. It starts with the release after this one, so grab v0.50.0 from the website.
🚂 **Add to Steam**: Settings ▸ More ▸ Add to Steam puts the game in your Steam library with its art.
🎮 **Your mods on Discord**: in the desktop app, a Mod Store pack now shows its own villain and hero on your Discord profile instead of only the logo.
🔴 **Mod updates**: the Title's Mod Store button shows how many of your mods have an update.
🔗 **Get the game**: the button on your Discord profile now opens the downloads on the website.
🌐 **Steam script on the website**: on a computer, the website's top offers the Steam script right beside the download.

Thank you, Patatasaurio, Neptune (Typhon00) and PermaKarma, whose Gwenpool, Mephisto, Carnage and The Punisher packs the Discord fix was checked with, and every Mod Store creator!
