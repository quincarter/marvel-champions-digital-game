# v0.50.0: Awesome Mix, Now Playing 🎧

## 🎵 Every scenario has a soundtrack

43 new tracks: Kingpin, all three Shadowland scenarios, Breakout, Kang, Absorbing Man, Zola, and Civil War's Captain Marvel, She-Hulk and Vision get battle, victory and defeat songs. Fear No Evil and Shadowland get an interlude and a finale, and Stop the Presses! and Crossbones get new takes. That's 221 songs in Extras ▸ Music, each opened as you earn it.

## ⚡ Updates that install themselves

The desktop game now updates itself from any installer: Update downloads while you play, Restart applies it. It starts with the next release, so grab v0.50.0 from the website. And Settings ▸ More ▸ Add to Steam puts the game in your Steam library with its art.

## 🎬 Replays that enter like a boss fight

A shared replay link opens on its own loading screen, then a versus screen of the villain against your heroes. Every replay opens that way now.

## 🎮 Your mods, on Discord and the Title

A Mod Store pack shows its own villain and hero on your Discord profile. The Title's Mod Store button counts your mods with updates, and on Linux mod downloads show their progress.

## 🦾 You asked, we fixed

Power Man's chi counters ask a clear question, with buttons you can see. Thanks for the report, Merman-Helville!

## 🔗 Also in v0.50.0

Get the game on your Discord profile opens the website's downloads, and on a computer the website offers the Steam script beside the download.

Thank you, Patatasaurio, Neptune (Typhon00) and PermaKarma, whose packs the Discord fix was checked with!
