## Lights down. ¡Cartas en español!

Dark mode is here: tap the moon at the top of any screen, or turn on Settings ▸ Display ▸ Dark mode, and the light screens and popups turn warm charcoal while your card art stays exactly as printed (#459, asked for by @neopolii). Every installer and the APK now also comes in Spanish (look for -ES- in the file name), with 4,053 of the 4,312 cards in their Spanish printing from alaintxu's mc-ocr scans, and the web version switches language with a globe button (#337, asked for by @AntonioAPG). Part 5 of the rules audit by @bluefireF5ran brings a new response window and seven more rulings (#410). Thanks to @zackburnley (#478, #479, #480), @neopolii (#458, #485), @andy-xxvii27 (#462) and @AlecHolland1582 (#496) for the reports: the Trophy Room folds and sorts, shelves keep coasting after a flick, the back gesture steps back a screen, the deck builder fills a wide window, each hero gets their own Basic upgrade after Zola, and Spider-Man, Captain Marvel, Black Panther and Nebula get clean new scans.
