## Back on Track

A small release with two fixes. On Android, the back gesture and the back button step back a screen again (#998, #999). And the Mod Store page of "Don't Make Me Angry!", the house rule with a starter deck, shows the art of its 30 official cards again (#1000). Thank you, Abis Mal and @bluefireF5ran, for the pack.
