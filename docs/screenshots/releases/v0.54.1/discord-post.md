# v0.54.1: Back on Track 🔙

A small patch with two fixes.

🤖 **Android**: the back gesture and the back button work again. They step back a screen, close an open sheet, open Pause in a game and send the app to the background from the title screen.
🧩 **Mod Store**: the page of "Don't Make Me Angry!", the house rule with a starter deck, shows the art of its 30 official cards again, and card hover and Inspect open the full card.

Thank you, Abis Mal and bluefireF5ran for "Don't Make Me Angry!", and Android players for your patience!
