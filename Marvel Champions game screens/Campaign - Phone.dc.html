CAMPAIGN — PHONE
The Rise of Red Skull (MC10), told as a five-issue comic. Board and in-game screens are unchanged: Screens - Phone. Other sizes: Tablet · Desktop.
ISSUES, NOT A MENUFive issues in printed order. No scenario picker; the next one is always the only one.
SIGN ONCEHeroes are signed to the roster at the start and locked for the whole run.
THE LOG KEEPS ITSELFEvery record step resolves automatically. Players only see real choices.
REWARDS ARE SCENESAn upgrade is handed to you by someone in the story, not picked from a list.
LOSING IS A REWINDStandard retry is free, so it reads as “that's not how it happened”.
EVERY ENTRY HAS A “SO WHAT”Each log line says what it will do and in which issue.
