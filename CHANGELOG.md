### Engine
* Wave 5 schema: lettered main scheme stages with environment faces, negative Victory, scheme icons on any card, progressing identities (refused until built), Sinister Six set-aside villains and card-ability win
* Villains that start set aside, come back and are set aside again; the active counter moves by activation order (The Sinister Six)
* An enemy activation can be interrupted and canceled, and the villain's activation is announced with no villain in play (Web Binding, Ambush!)
* Several main schemes with one marked by a counter (Venom Goblin's glider), moving counters, and a main scheme stage flipping to its environment face
* Acceleration tokens on any card count toward step one, move between cards, and announce their placement (Tracking Prey, Hapless Pedestrians)
* Encounter cards in a player's deck, hand and discard pile: moveCards into a player's zones, an interrupt when one is drawn or discarded from a deck, and a response after a boost card resolves (Mysterio)
* Boost cards held on a card that never activates (an identity) and moved to an enemy by card text (Venom, Leave Us Alone!)
* A resolveSpecials with bind reports what the resolved Specials bound, such as the cards Surging Sands discarded (Sandslide)
* A constant that increases the damage a character takes, once per damage event (Bell Tower)
* A constant that makes a card count as another card type with a trait for query matching (Festering Mass)
* Scheme icons printed on cards that are not schemes count toward hazard, crisis and acceleration (Venom ally, Public Outcry, Team Leader)
* A mode-of-play predicate, and an encounter card's grants to itself apply while it is revealed (Surprise!, Frequent Flyers)
* Every attack of a player's turn is recorded, and a predicate reads whether this is the first attack this turn (Venom III)
* When/After a card leaves play: an event with interrupt and response windows, answered by the card that left and matched on its traits as it left (Spider-Man (Hobie Brown), Web of Life and Destiny)
* Max 1 per [instance]: one use per triggering event shared by every copy of the card's title (Web-Bracelet, Ghost Kick, Phantom Flip)
* A card played from facedown is played faceup, and a player's own facedown attachment is visible to them (George Stacy)
* A card's payment records how many resources each resource ability generated for it (VEN#m, Rapid Deployment)
* A hand resource card can permit spending it toward another player's payment while a condition holds (Everyday Hero)
* A card in hand can be excluded from the hand-size count: discard down, draw up and the mulligan draw ignore it (Connection to the Worldmind)
* A character may hold any number of tough status cards (Armadillo)
* Printed resources of the cards in a player's hand can be treated as another type, for payment and every printed-resource count (Haywire)
* An additional cost to thwart a scheme, resources or indirect damage, asked before each thwart of it (Giant Monster Attack, Cat in a Tree)
* A basic thwart limited to the schemes a rule names, and ignores that apply to basic thwarts only (Retinal Display)
* Progressing identities: the first version is seated with the others set aside, and swapping to the next version keeps the dial, counters and statuses (Ironheart)
* Setup: a player can be given additional mulligans (PlayerSetup.extraMulligans), each a full second mulligan decided right after the first, for Sinister Motives reputation node 5 (docs/phase7-wave5.md §3.26)
* Optional encounter-card abilities triggered by damage are offered to the player dealing it (Bell Tower's Quiet side, docs/phase7-wave5.md §3.29 and §4 Q8); replacing an attack's damage with counters is tested to deal no damage and no excess (MC27 p. 21 FAQ)
* Generating resources is an event abilities can respond to (M.O.R.B.I.U.S.), and a resource ability can spend counters once per use, several times in one payment (Spider-Ham's toon counters)
* Campaign queries for MC27's reputation conditions and Waking Nightmare (accelerationTokensInPlay, defeatedIdentities, cardsInPlayerDecks, playersInScenario) and a grantAdditionalMulligans effect for reputation node 5 (docs/phase7-wave5.md §3.27)
* A permanent attachment whose host leaves play stays in play, unattached, in its controller's play area, and on.attachedCardDefeated() for a minion or side scheme host (Wrist Navigator)
* Looking at the top card of the encounter deck at any time (Sector Scan): a lasting mayLookAtTopOfEncounterDeck rule, read by faceVisible for the named viewer only; Inspect shows the face to that seat (docs/phase7-wave5.md §3.28)
* Sinister Motives' additional mulligan is a second pass in player order after every player's normal mulligan (p1, p2, then p1, p2); a player who kept their hand is not offered it (docs/phase7-wave5.md §4.1 Q19, Q20)

* A printed text box that cannot be blanked: RuleSpec textBoxCannotBeBlanked for SP//dr Suit and SP//dr (docs/phase7-wave5.md §3.31)

* A permanent encounter attachment with no player owner or controller is discarded to its encounter discard pile when its host leaves play; player cards still return unattached (docs/phase7-wave5.md §4.2 Q26)
