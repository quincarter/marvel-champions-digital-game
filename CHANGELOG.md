### Engine
* Villain defeat is an interruptible event ("when the villain would be defeated … instead"), RuleSpec cannotBeDefeated, ∞ hit points, and a flip to or from an ∞ face resets the dial (The Collector, Hela, MaGog)
* Round timing points as trigger events: phaseBeginning, phaseEnding (the villain phase's end is the round's end) and villainStepResolved ("after resolving step one of the villain phase"), pushed only when an ability listens
* Cycle 2 keywords enforced: Hinder X and Uses with per player values, Victory X and the victory display, Patrol, the amplify icon, and stalwart (plus RuleSpec cannotHaveStatus) shedding statuses as it applies
* RuleSpec firstRevealGainsSurge and a per-round reveal history: "the first [card] revealed each round/phase gains surge" (Nebula, Mister Knife), read as the card is revealed
* EffectSpec defeat (a character defeated outright), addCounters upTo/bind ("to a maximum of X", local), ValueSpec min/max/dealtEncounterCount, playedThisRound without a card type, and an event its own ability moves is no longer discarded
* A tough status resolves before "would take damage" interrupts (status-card timing priority), and constant limits on damage taken: RuleSpec reduceDamageTaken and maxDamageTakenPerAttack
* The first player's card and First Player abilities: RuleSpec controlledByFirstPlayer (the Milano follows the first player token), firstPlayerOnly on actions/interrupts/responses, and resource abilities usable by any player
* Lasting "each time …" effects (eachTimeUntil), attacks that deal indirect damage (attacksDealIndirectDamage), an excess damage bonus (excessDamageBonus), and a variable cost of any resource type with a maximum
* Player elimination resolves a permanent attachment's "attach to" text and removes other permanent cards it does not own (RRG step 3); RuleSpec cannotBeUnattached (the Power Stone)
* EffectSpec cancelConsequentialDamage ("does not take consequential damage for this use", Cosmo); consequential damage events are marked
* Star-Lord's cost reduction: AbilityDefinition.playCostReduction used through playCard.costReductionAbilities, and AbilityCost.dealEncounterCards ("deal yourself 1 facedown encounter card →")
* The Collection: a scenario out-of-play area (ZoneId scenarioArea, createScenarioArea, a destination, selector and count) and RuleSpec discardFromPlayDestination redirecting cards discarded from play
* Gamora and Venom: IdentityDeckbuilding.offAspectAllowance ("up to 6 attack and/or thwart events from other aspects"), RuleSpec restrictedLimit, and Predicate playedThisTurn
* AbilityCost.spendCounters can now spend counters off a target other than the ability's own card
* excessDamageBonus is now exported for card-side tests to read directly
* Support putIntoPlay for ownerless scenario-specific supports, currentActivationIs predicate, boost-count adjustment during a card's own Boost ability
* Ability DSL/engine primitives for Gamora (threatCannotBeRemoved/cannotAttack player scoping, zone.bottommostOnly, TargetQuery.unique)
* discardFromPlayDestination gains thenPlaceThreat so a single Forced Interrupt can redirect a discard and place follow-up threat
* Cost modifiers now apply to a card played only at its own trigger window (payForCard), matching normal play pricing
* A basic defense's 'after you use a basic power' responses now wait for the attack to end (RRG 1.8 p. 16); new DSL builders eachTimeUntil and on.youDealDamage close the Lashing Vines, Deft Focus, Schadenfreude and Salvage gaps (docs/phase7-wave3.md §3.28–§3.31)
* Team-Up names as vocabulary: TargetQuery.titled and identitySetTitled read a card's own Team-Up keyword (DSL teamUpCharacters, ofTeamUpSet); the Team-Up play and deck checks now accept 'Hero/Alter-ego' names such as Heart of the Panther's (docs/phase7-wave3.md §3.34)
* mainSchemeStageNumber value, encounterSetAside move destination (for Escape the Museum)
* PlayerRef superlative ('the player engaged with the fewest minions', Drang III) and choosePlayer.among for its ties, which go to the first player on encounter cards (RRG 1.8 p. 19); DSL superlativePlayer (docs/phase7-wave3.md §3.35)
* Three cost primitives: 'remove up to N counters' sized by the player (spendCounters.upTo, bind), 'discard the top card of your deck' (discardFromDeck, with the empty-deck rules), and either/or costs (AbilityCost.either); plus AbilityLimit.per 'player' and optional Command.costSelection / LegalAction.costBranches / costCounters (docs/phase7-wave3.md §3.32, §3.33, §3.36)
* MainSchemeStage.completionLoses: completing a non-final stage that prints 'If this stage is completed, the players lose the game' now loses instead of advancing (Escape the Museum 1B/2B; Kang's Arrival 1B once re-emitted); first tests for indirect damage 'among players' (docs/phase7-wave3.md §3.37, §3.38)
* engine: resolveSpecials inherits the calling ability's controller, so a Special ability's own 'you' (an encounter card with no controller of its own) isn't null
* PlayerRef controllerOf and TargetQuery.hasAttachment: the player who controls a card, and a character with a matching attachment (Single-Minded Fury, Target Practice)
* divide 'up to' (Agile Flight) and a play restriction on any condition, constant.playOnlyIf, with ValueSpec victoryDisplayCount (Sliding Shot and every other 'Play only if' wording)
* AbilityCost.sameResourceType: 'Spend N resources of the same type' (Kree Combat Armor); a timing window's ability payment now gets the same resource checks as an action's
* An ally's consequential damage carries the results of the basic attack or thwart it follows (attack.*/thwart.*), for Martyr's 'if that attack defeated an enemy'
* A defeat's destination: setDefeatDestination (Regroup's 'return it to its owner's hand instead of discarding it'), RuleSpec defeatDestination generalizing Time Portal's rule, and characterDefeated.fromAttack
* An identity's own defeat now carries the same fromAttack/sourceInstanceId hint an ally's already did; fixes Judge, Jury, Executioner's overbroad 'defeated by an enemy attack' trigger (ron)
* A turn beginning opens an interrupt window, and an uncontrolled attachment's or obligation's "your" names its host's controller or its play area's player; scripts The Poison's Forced Interrupt (gmw 16125).
* New EffectSpec dealAsEncounterCard deals an already-identified card to a player as a facedown encounter card; scripts You Dare Oppose Me? (ron 90005), completing the Kree Fanatic set.
* One effect dealing damage to several characters deals it simultaneously, and a villain defeated at the same time as the last identity is a loss (FFG rulings May 18, 2023 and June 2, 2026; wave 3 §4 Q1).
* An enemy can attack another enemy (EffectSpec enemyAttacksEnemy, docs/phase7-wave3.md §3.23): an attack, not an activation, per the user's §4 Q12 decision. Moondragon (19013) is scripted, and drax is fully scripted.
* An effect's "up to N" now chooses at least 1 whenever a legal target exists, per the user's §4 Q16 decision: divide.upTo (Agile Flight), the new chooseTarget.upTo, and the "up to" chooseCards scripts. chooseTarget.optional now means only a printed "may".
* Close campaign-foundation gaps GMW needs: composeEncounterSets' set-aside pool, a unit-price collection filter, cardsInScenarioArea, and seatCount/divide/valueAtLeast arithmetic
* TargetQuery.printedId, for two cards that print the same title (MC16's Campaign Challenge side-scheme faces)
* CampaignGameQuery keywordValueSum and capAt, for a summed printed keyword value with a printed ceiling
* CampaignGameQuery atMost, the complement of atLeast
* Engine: reorderCards can place each card on the top or bottom of the encounter deck (chooseBottomCards, orderCards to encounterDeckBottom), and AbilityCost.conditional lets the board pick a cost branch (costIf, costAsDetermined)
* Card selection can take at most N matches (CardSelector atMost, DSL atMost/oneCopyOf) for 'search for one copy' instructions
* Campaign elimination policy: a player defeated in a won expert game skips that scenario's Victory steps (MC16 p. 5); GMW rejoins them at printed HP (ruling June 2, 2026 (3))
* An obligation drawn from a player deck (The Rise of Red Skull's expert campaign obligations) goes into that player's play area instead of their hand; refills to hand size keep drawing past it, and only that player can use its abilities.
* An obligation drawn at setup (opening hand or mulligan) goes into play and is not replaced by that draw; the mulligan draws up to the (possibly reduced) hand size (maintainer decision, docs/campaign-mode-design.md Q20).
